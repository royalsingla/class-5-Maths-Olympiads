# Week 9 — Reasoning: Patterns, Analogy, Coding & Word Puzzles

Patterns, analogy, classification, alphabet test, coding–decoding,
logical sequence of words, and puzzle tests. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ,
untimed.

---

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Guitar  B) Violin  C) Tabla  D) Chair

**Q2.** Doctor is to Patient as Teacher is to ___.
A) School  B) Student  C) Classroom  D) Book

**Q3.** Find the odd one out:
A) 16  B) 25  C) 30  D) 49

**Q4.** If each letter of a word is moved 3 steps forward in the alphabet (A→D, B→E, …), CAT becomes FDW. Using the same rule, how is DOG written?
A) GRJ  B) GRI  C) FRJ  D) GQJ

**Q5.** Complete the series: B, D, F, H, ___
A) I  B) J  C) K  D) L

**Q6.** Arrange in dictionary (alphabetical) order: 1. Mango  2. Monkey  3. Market  4. Moon
A) 1, 3, 2, 4  B) 1, 2, 3, 4  C) 3, 1, 2, 4  D) 1, 3, 4, 2

**Q7.** Find the odd one out:
A) Triangle  B) Square  C) Pentagon  D) Sphere

**Q8.** Pen is to Write as Scissors is to ___.
A) Paper  B) Cut  C) Sharp  D) Blade

**Q9.** In a certain code, FLOWER is written as HNQYGT (each letter moved 2 steps forward). How is GARDEN written in the same code?
A) ICTFGP  B) ICTFGO  C) ICTEGP  D) IDTFGP

**Q10.** Find the missing number: 6, 13, 27, 55, ___
A) 105  B) 108  C) 111  D) 115

**Q11.** Find the odd one out:
A) Cow  B) Goat  C) Hen  D) Sheep

**Q12.** 5 friends — Priya, Simran, Raj, Meena, and Tina — are sitting in a row. Priya is at the left end. Simran is immediately to the right of Priya. Raj is immediately to the right of Simran. Tina is at the right end. Who is sitting between Raj and Tina?
A) Priya  B) Simran  C) Meena  D) Raj

**Q13.** Find the odd one out:
A) Red  B) Blue  C) Green  D) Rainbow

**Q14.** Complete the series: Z, X, V, T, R, ___
A) O  B) P  C) Q  D) N

**Q15.** If 'A' stands for '+', 'B' stands for '−', 'C' stands for '×', and 'D' stands for '÷', find the value of: 12 C 3 A 8 D 4
A) 36  B) 38  C) 40  D) 42
