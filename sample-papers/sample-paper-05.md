# Sample Paper 5 — Class 5 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 50 questions, 60 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. This is your Level‑1 dress rehearsal — treat it like
the real exam: set a timer, no notes, no calculator.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Lion  B) Tiger  C) Leopard  D) Peacock

**Q2.** Thermometer is to Temperature as Speedometer is to ___.
A) Distance  B) Speed  C) Time  D) Direction

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 3 steps forward in the alphabet, BOOK becomes ERRN. Using the same rule, how is DESK written?
A) GHVN  B) GHVM  C) GHUN  D) FHVN

**Q4.** Complete the series: 11, 22, 44, 88, ___
A) 132  B) 154  C) 176  D) 198

**Q5.** In a row of 35 students, Ritu is 19th from the left. What is her position from the right?
A) 16th  B) 17th  C) 18th  D) 15th

**Q6.** Facing South, Karan turns 90° anticlockwise, then another 90° anticlockwise. Which direction does he face now?
A) East  B) West  C) North  D) South

**Q7.** Arrange in a logical order of a plant's growth: 1. Seedling  2. Seed  3. Flowering Plant  4. Sapling
A) 2, 1, 4, 3  B) 1, 2, 3, 4  C) 2, 4, 1, 3  D) 1, 2, 4, 3

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 33  B) 44  C) 53  D) 66

**Q9.** If today is Tuesday, what day will it be after 45 days?
A) Thursday  B) Friday  C) Saturday  D) Sunday

**Q10.** Pointing to a girl, Suresh said, "She is the daughter of my father's only sister." How is the girl related to Suresh?
A) Sister  B) Cousin  C) Niece  D) Daughter

**Q11.** Find the missing number: 7, 15, 31, 63, ___
A) 119  B) 123  C) 127  D) 131

**Q12.** In a certain code, each letter is moved 2 steps backward in the alphabet, so FLOWER is written as DJMUCP. How is GARDEN written in the same code?
A) EYPBCL  B) EXPBCL  C) EYPBCK  D) FYPBCL

**Q13.** Find the odd one out:
A) Pyramid  B) Cone  C) Rectangle  D) Cylinder

**Q14.** In a race between four runners — A, B, C, D — A finished before B but after C. D finished last. Who finished first?
A) A  B) B  C) C  D) D

**Q15.** Complete the series: 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ___
A) 36  B) 37  C) 38  D) 39

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35, 1 mark each)

**Q16.** 46,789 + 27,345 = ?
A) 74,134  B) 74,034  C) 73,134  D) 74,234

**Q17.** 80,006 − 39,478 = ?
A) 40,528  B) 40,428  C) 40,628  D) 41,528

**Q18.** 5,678 × 6 = ?
A) 34,068  B) 33,968  C) 34,168  D) 33,068

**Q19.** 57,834 ÷ 6 = ?
A) 9,639  B) 9,629  C) 9,649  D) 9,739

**Q20.** Find the HCF of 54 and 72.
A) 9  B) 12  C) 18  D) 36

**Q21.** Find the LCM of 14 and 21.
A) 42  B) 84  C) 21  D) 294

**Q22.** Which fraction is equivalent to 2/9?
A) 4/18  B) 9/2  C) 2/18  D) 4/9

**Q23.** 6/11 + 3/11 + 1/11 = ?
A) 10/11  B) 10/33  C) 9/11  D) 1

**Q24.** Convert 0.64 to a fraction in simplest form.
A) 16/25  B) 8/25  C) 32/25  D) 4/25

**Q25.** What is 55% of 220?
A) 111  B) 116  C) 121  D) 126

**Q26.** Express 13/25 as a percentage.
A) 48%  B) 50%  C) 52%  D) 54%

**Q27.** Two numbers are in the ratio 4:11. If the smaller is 16, find the larger.
A) 40  B) 42  C) 44  D) 46

**Q28.** The cost price of an item is ₹850 and the selling price is ₹680. Find the loss percentage.
A) 15%  B) 20%  C) 25%  D) 30%

**Q29.** Find the simple interest on ₹9,000 at 5% per annum for 4 years.
A) ₹1,700  B) ₹1,750  C) ₹1,800  D) ₹1,850

**Q30.** Convert 7 kg 8 g into grams.
A) 7,008  B) 7,080  C) 7,800  D) 708

**Q31.** Convert 3.25 hours into minutes.
A) 185  B) 190  C) 195  D) 200

**Q32.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 42 cm and breadth 23 cm.
A) 65 cm  B) 130 cm  C) 260 cm  D) 966 cm

**Q33.** Find the area of a square with side 17 cm.
A) 279 sq cm  B) 289 sq cm  C) 68 sq cm  D) 299 sq cm

**Q34.** How many lines of symmetry does a square have?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 4  D) 8

**Q35.** How many right angles make three-quarters of a full turn?
A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 6

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45, 1 mark each)

**Q36.** A factory makes 3,400 toys each day. How many toys does it make in 16 days?
A) 52,400  B) 53,400  C) 54,400  D) 55,400

**Q37.** Manoj had ₹6,400. He spent ₹1,950 and then ₹2,175. How much money does he have left?
A) ₹2,175  B) ₹2,275  C) ₹2,375  D) ₹2,475

**Q38.** A tank holds 810 L. If 4/9 of it is used, how much water is left?
A) 430 L  B) 440 L  C) 450 L  D) 460 L

**Q39.** 27 kg of flour costs ₹1,215. Find the cost of 1 kg.
A) ₹40  B) ₹42  C) ₹45  D) ₹48

**Q40.** 17 rows of plants have 19 plants each. How many plants are there in total?
A) 313  B) 323  C) 333  D) 343

**Q41.** A woman deposits ₹6,000 at 7% simple interest per year. How much interest will she earn in 5 years?
A) ₹2,000  B) ₹2,050  C) ₹2,100  D) ₹2,150

**Q42.** A shopkeeper bought goods for ₹2,800 and sold them for ₹3,304. Find the profit percentage.
A) 15%  B) 18%  C) 20%  D) 22%

**Q43.** A bus travels at 63 km/h. How far will it travel in 1 hour 20 minutes?
A) 78 km  B) 80 km  C) 84 km  D) 88 km

**Q44.** A recipe needs 3/10 cup of butter. How much butter is needed for 20 such recipes?
A) 5 cups  B) 6 cups  C) 7 cups  D) 8 cups

**Q45.** In a survey of 350 students, 40% like dance. How many students like dance?
A) 130  B) 135  C) 140  D) 145

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q46–Q50, 3 marks each)

**Q46.** A number is increased by 45% to get 435. Find the original number.
A) 280  B) 290  C) 300  D) 310

**Q47.** The sum of five consecutive numbers is 150. Find the middle number.
A) 28  B) 29  C) 30  D) 31

**Q48.** The simple interest on a sum for 8 years at 3% per annum is ₹1,680. Find the sum.
A) ₹6,500  B) ₹7,000  C) ₹7,500  D) ₹8,000

**Q49.** Vikram had some money. He spent 1/6 of it on snacks and 2/5 of the remainder on books. He is left with ₹1,500. How much money did he have at first?
A) ₹2,700  B) ₹2,850  C) ₹3,000  D) ₹3,150

**Q50.** Two numbers are in the ratio 3:4. If each is decreased by 5, the new ratio becomes 2:3. Find the larger of the two original numbers.
A) 16  B) 18  C) 20  D) 22
