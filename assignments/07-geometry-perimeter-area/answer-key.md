# Week 7 — Geometry, Symmetry, Perimeter & Area — Answer Key

**Q1. C) 8** — An octagon has 8 sides.

**Q2. B) Circle** — A circle is a curved shape with no straight sides at all.

**Q3. C) 3** — An equilateral triangle has 3 lines of symmetry, one through each vertex and the midpoint of the opposite side.

**Q4. B) Right** — A right angle measures exactly 90°.

**Q5. C) Obtuse** — An obtuse angle is between 90° and 180°.

**Q6. B) 60 cm** — Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4 × 15 = 60 cm.

**Q7. B) 72 cm** — Perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth) = 2 × (22+14) = 72 cm.

**Q8. B) 12 cm** — Side = Perimeter ÷ 4 = 48 ÷ 4 = 12 cm.

**Q9. B) 198 sq cm** — Area = length × breadth = 18 × 11 = 198 sq cm.

**Q10. B) 256 sq cm** — Area = side × side = 16 × 16 = 256 sq cm.

**Q11. B) 12 m** — Breadth = Area ÷ length = 168 ÷ 14 = 12 m.

**Q12. B) 2** — A straight angle is 180°; 180 ÷ 90 = 2.

**Q13. B) Cone** — A cone has one curved surface and one flat circular base.

**Q14. D) 12** — A cuboid has 12 edges.

**Q15. C) 96 m** — The path adds 2 m on every side, so the outer square's side = 20 + 2 + 2 = 24 m; perimeter = 4 × 24 = 96 m.
