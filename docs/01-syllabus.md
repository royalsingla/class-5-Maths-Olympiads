# Syllabus — Class 5 Mathematics Olympiad

This maps the topic list for **SOF IMO Class 5** (primary reference)
against **Silverzone iOM** and **Unified Council UIMO**. Compared to Class 4,
all three boards converge on the same step up: bigger numbers, and the
introduction of percentage, ratio & proportion, and profit/loss.

## 1. SOF IMO — Class 5 (primary reference)

### Section 1 — Logical Reasoning
- Patterns (number & figure series)
- Analogy and Classification (odd one out)
- Alphabet Test
- Coding–Decoding
- Ranking Test
- Mirror and Water Images
- Geometrical Shapes
- Embedded Figures
- Direction Sense Test
- Logical Sequence of Words
- Puzzle Test
- Clock and Calendar

(Two sub-topics are new versus Class 4: **Water Images** and **Logical
Sequence of Words** / **Puzzle Test**, both verbal/sequencing puzzles
rather than visual ones.)

### Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning
- Numerals and Number Names, Number Sense (7–8 digit numbers, Indian
  place-value system: lakhs and crores)
- Roman Numerals
- Computation Operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
- Factors and Multiples, HCF and LCM, number patterns
- Fractions and Decimals: comparing, equivalent, operations, conversions
- **Percentage, Ratio and Proportion** (new vs. Class 4)
- **Profit and Loss, and an introduction to Simple Interest** (new vs. Class 4)
- Measurement: Length, Weight, Capacity, Time, Temperature, Money
- Geometry: shapes, angles, symmetry
- Perimeter of various shapes; Area of rectangle and square
- Data Handling

### Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics
- Same syllabus as Section 2, framed as real-life word problems.

### Section 4 — Achievers Section (HOTS)
- Higher Order Thinking Questions drawn from the Section 2 syllabus —
  usually 2–3 step problems, worth more marks per question than any other
  section (see `02-exam-pattern.md`).

## 2. Cross-olympiad topic map

| Topic area | SOF IMO | Silverzone iOM | Unified Council UIMO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Number sense (7–8 digit numbers), Roman numerals | ✅ Sec. 2 | ✅ "Numbers and Their Operations" | ✅ "Large Numbers" |
| Four operations, factors/multiples, HCF/LCM | ✅ Sec. 2/3 | ✅ | ✅ "Factors and Multiples" |
| Fractions & decimals | ✅ Sec. 2/3 | ✅ "Parts and Whole" | ✅ |
| **Percentage, ratio & proportion** | ✅ Sec. 2/3 (new) | ✅ dedicated unit (new) | ✅ "Ratios ... Conversions and Percentages" (new) |
| **Profit, loss & simple interest** | ✅ Sec. 2/3 (new) | — (light) | ✅ "Profit and Loss", "Simple Interest" (new) |
| Measurement (length/weight/capacity/time/temperature), money | ✅ Sec. 2/3 | ✅ | ✅ |
| Geometry, perimeter & area | ✅ Sec. 2/3 | ✅ "Geometry and Measurement" | ✅ |
| Data handling | ✅ Sec. 2/3 | ✅ "Graphical Representation of Data" | — (light) |
| Pure logical reasoning (coding, analogy, mirror/water images, direction sense, puzzle test) | ✅ Sec. 1 (dedicated, 15 Q) | ✅ "Reasoning and Aptitude" | ✅ "Logical Reasoning" (10 marks) |
| Higher-order / critical thinking | ✅ Sec. 4 "Achievers" | ✅ "Scholar's Zone" | ✅ "Critical Thinking" |

**Takeaway:** as with Class 4, thoroughly practising the SOF IMO Class 5
list covers essentially all of Silverzone iOM and UIMO as well — new for
Class 5, specifically, is percentage/ratio/proportion and
profit-loss/simple-interest, which now get their own practice week (see
`03-practice-plan.md`).
