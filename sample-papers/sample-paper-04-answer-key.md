# Sample Paper 4 — Answer Key

Total: 60 marks (45 × 1 mark + 5 × 3 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **D) Carrot** — Rose, Lily, and Lotus are flowers; Carrot is a vegetable.
2. **B) Account** — A key opens a lock; a password opens (access to) an account.
3. **A) HFU** — The rule is "+5 letters": C→H, A→F, P→U.
4. **C) 144** — Each term doubles: 9, 18, 36, 72, 144.
5. **B) 13th** — Position from the right = 28 − 16 + 1 = 13.
6. **C) East** — Two 90° clockwise turns from West = 180° turn = East.
7. **A) 1, 2, 4, 3** — A butterfly's life cycle: Egg → Caterpillar → Cocoon → Butterfly.
8. **C) 25** — 14, 21, 35 are multiples of 7; 25 is not.
9. **B) Friday** — 40 days = 5 full weeks (35 days, same day: Sunday) + 5 more days → Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri.
10. **B) Uncle** — Priya's mother's sister is her maternal aunt; the aunt's husband is Priya's uncle.
11. **C) 95** — The pattern is "double and add 1": 5×2+1=11, 11×2+1=23, 23×2+1=47, 47×2+1=95.
12. **A) RNCPV** — The rule is "+2 letters": P→R, L→N, A→C, N→P, T→V.
13. **C) Sphere** — Circle, Oval, and Square are flat (2-D) shapes; a Sphere is a solid (3-D) shape.
14. **D) W** — W > X > Y > Z, so W is the heaviest.
15. **D) 21** — Each term is the sum of the two before it (a Fibonacci-style pattern): 8+13=21.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

16. **A) 95,245** — 78,456 + 16,789 = 95,245.
17. **A) 32,346** — 60,004 − 27,658 = 32,346.
18. **A) 30,312** — 3,789 × 8 = 30,312.
19. **A) 8,554** — 68,432 ÷ 8 = 8,554.
20. **C) 20** — HCF of 40 (2³×5) and 60 (2²×3×5) is 2²×5 = 20.
21. **A) 30** — Multiples of 10: 10,20,30... Multiples of 15: 15,30... First common one = 30.
22. **A) 14/20** — Multiply top and bottom of 7/10 by 2.
23. **A) 7/9** — Same denominator: 4+2+1=7, so 7/9.
24. **A) 8/25** — 0.32 = 32/100, which simplifies (÷4) to 8/25.
25. **C) 60** — 40% of 150 = 0.40 × 150 = 60.
26. **B) 55%** — 11/20 = 0.55 = 55%.
27. **C) 45** — 1 part = 35 ÷ 7 = 5; larger = 9 parts = 45.
28. **B) 25%** — Profit = 750−600 = ₹150; profit % = 150/600 × 100 = 25%.
29. **C) ₹1,680** — SI = (7000×4×6)÷100 = ₹1,680.
30. **A) 6,045** — 6 kg = 6,000 g; + 45 g = 6,045 g.
31. **C) 3 h 45 min** — 225 ÷ 60 = 3 remainder 45.
32. **B) 96 cm** — Perimeter = 2×(31+17) = 96 cm.
33. **B) 169 sq cm** — Area = 13×13 = 169 sq cm.
34. **D) Countless (infinite)** — A circle can be folded along any diameter, so it has infinitely many lines of symmetry.
35. **C) 8** — Two full turns = 720°; 720÷90 = 8.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

36. **C) 1,10,000** — 2,750 × 40 = 1,10,000.
37. **B) ₹2,860** — 7200 − 2450 − 1890 = 2,860.
38. **C) 360 L** — 5/8 of 960 = 600 L used; 960 − 600 = 360 L left.
39. **C) ₹50** — 1,600 ÷ 32 = 50.
40. **B) 322** — 14 × 23 = 322.
41. **C) ₹1,800** — SI = (4500×8×5)÷100 = ₹1,800.
42. **B) 15%** — Profit = 2415−2100 = ₹315; profit % = 315/2100 × 100 = 15%.
43. **C) 51 km** — 2 h 50 min = 17/6 hours; 18 × 17/6 = 51 km.
44. **B) 7 cups** — 7/8 × 8 = 7 cups.
45. **C) 168** — 42% of 400 = 0.42 × 400 = 168.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (3 marks each)

46. **C) 600** — If the original number is *x*: 0.65*x* = 390 → *x* = 600.
47. **C) 28** — Four consecutive numbers *n*, *n*+1, *n*+2, *n*+3 sum to 4*n*+6 = 106 → *n* = 25; the largest is 25+3 = 28.
48. **C) ₹9,000** — SI = (P×5×6)÷100 = 0.3×P = 2,700 → P = 2,700÷0.3 = ₹9,000.
49. **B) ₹2,000** — After clothes, 5/8 of the money remains. After books (1/5 of that spent), 4/5 of 5/8 = 1/2 of the original remains. So 1/2 of the total = ₹1,000 → total = ₹2,000.
50. **B) 40** — Let the numbers be 5*x* and 7*x*. (5*x*+8)/(7*x*+8) = 3/4 → 4(5*x*+8) = 3(7*x*+8) → 20*x*+32 = 21*x*+24 → *x* = 8. Smaller number = 5×8 = 40.
