# Week 5 — Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Profit/Loss & Simple Interest

New topics versus Class 4 — take this week a little slower. 15 questions,
4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** What is 20% of 350?
A) 60  B) 65  C) 70  D) 75

**Q2.** Express 2/5 as a percentage.
A) 25%  B) 40%  C) 45%  D) 52%

**Q3.** 45 is what percent of 180?
A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 30%  D) 35%

**Q4.** The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 5:4. If there are 45 boys, how many girls are there?
A) 32  B) 34  C) 36  D) 40

**Q5.** Divide ₹600 between A and B in the ratio 2:3. Find B's share.
A) ₹240  B) ₹300  C) ₹360  D) ₹400

**Q6.** If 3 pens cost ₹45, find the cost of 7 pens.
A) ₹95  B) ₹100  C) ₹105  D) ₹110

**Q7.** A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹250 and sells it for ₹300. Find the profit percentage.
A) 15%  B) 20%  C) 25%  D) 30%

**Q8.** A book is bought for ₹200 and sold at a loss of ₹40. Find the loss percentage.
A) 15%  B) 20%  C) 25%  D) 30%

**Q9.** Find the simple interest on ₹5,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years.
A) ₹1,000  B) ₹1,100  C) ₹1,200  D) ₹1,300

**Q10.** A sum of ₹2,000 earns ₹500 as simple interest in 5 years. Find the rate of interest per annum.
A) 4%  B) 5%  C) 6%  D) 7%

**Q11.** In a school, 480 students were surveyed and 60% said they like mathematics. How many students like mathematics?
A) 268  B) 278  C) 288  D) 298

**Q12.** If the cost price is ₹450 and the selling price is ₹405, find the loss percentage.
A) 5%  B) 8%  C) 10%  D) 12%

**Q13.** The ratio of two numbers is 4:7, and their sum is 121. Find the larger number.
A) 66  B) 70  C) 77  D) 84

**Q14.** A man invests ₹8,000 at 10% simple interest per year. Find the total amount (principal + interest) after 2 years.
A) ₹9,200  B) ₹9,400  C) ₹9,600  D) ₹9,800

**Q15.** A trader marks up an item's price by 25% above its cost price of ₹400, then gives a 20% discount on the marked price. Find the selling price.
A) ₹380  B) ₹400  C) ₹420  D) ₹450
