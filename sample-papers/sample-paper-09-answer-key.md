# Sample Paper 9 — Answer Key (Level‑2 Difficulty Step-Up)

Total: 60 marks (45 × 1 mark + 5 × 3 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **D) Chess** — Hockey, Football, and Cricket are played with a ball; Chess is a board game.
2. **A) Student** — A doctor treats a patient; a teacher teaches a student.
3. **A) IPSTF** — The rule is "+1 letter": H→I, O→P, R→S, S→T, E→F.
4. **C) 79** — Two patterns are interleaved: the odd-position terms go 5, 10, 15, 20 (+5 each); the even-position terms go 100, 93, 86, ? (−7 each) — so the next even term is 79.
5. **A) Vikas, Sameer, Tanvi, Umesh, Rhea** — Tanvi is at position 3 (middle). Vikas is at the left end, position 1. Umesh is immediately right of Tanvi, position 4. Sameer sits between Vikas (1) and Tanvi (3), so Sameer is position 2. The only person left, Rhea, takes position 5.
6. **B) 18 m North** — Both North legs add together (16 + 9 = 25 m North), and the 7 m South leg is subtracted: 25 − 7 = 18 m North.
7. **B) Tuesday** — 30 − 11 = 19 days later; 19 = 2 weeks + 5 days, so the day of the week moves forward 5 days from Thursday: Tuesday.
8. **D) 45** — 21, 28, and 42 are all multiples of 7; 45 is not.
9. **C) 1,200** — Each term is multiplied by one more than the last multiplier used (×2, ×4, ×5, ×6): 200 × 6 = 1,200.
10. **C) 14th** — Being 18th from the back means 17 people are closer to the back end than Farah. The 4 people who leave from the back end are among those 17, so her position from the back decreases by 4: 18 − 4 = 14th.
11. **C) 39** — 13, 29, and 43 are prime numbers; 39 (= 3×13) is not.
12. **A) SHQFLO** — The rule is "+3 letters": P→S, E→H, N→Q, C→F, I→L, L→O.
13. **D) Cylinder** — Pentagon, Hexagon, and Octagon are flat (2-D) shapes; a Cylinder is a solid (3-D) shape.
14. **D) Tina** — The finishing order is Sana, then Priya, then Rina, then Tina, so Tina finished last.
15. **B) 18** — The gaps between terms are 1, 2, 3, 4 — each one more than the last. The next gap is 5: 13 + 5 = 18.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

16. **A) 8,68,131** — 4,89,675 + 3,78,456 = 8,68,131.
17. **A) 2,70,325** — 7,60,000 − 4,89,675 = 2,70,325.
18. **A) 91,602** — 6,543 × 14 = 91,602.
19. **A) 5,432** — 97,776 ÷ 18 = 5,432 exactly.
20. **C) 36** — 108 = 2²×3³, 144 = 2⁴×3². HCF = 2²×3² = 36.
21. **C) 48** — 16 = 2⁴, 24 = 2³×3. LCM = 2⁴×3 = 48.
22. **D) 10/11** — As decimals: 8/9 ≈ 0.889, 5/6 ≈ 0.833, 13/15 ≈ 0.867, 10/11 ≈ 0.909. The greatest is 10/11.
23. **C) 11 km 150 m** — 6250 m + 4900 m = 11150 m = 11 km 150 m.
24. **C) 1,700 sq m** — Area = 50 × 34 = 1,700 sq m.
25. **C) 12 cm** — A regular nonagon has 9 equal sides: 108 ÷ 9 = 12 cm.
26. **C) 25%** — Profit = 2,250 − 1,800 = ₹450; profit % = 450/1800 × 100 = 25%.
27. **B) 10%** — If principal P doubles, the interest earned equals P itself. SI = (P×R×T)÷100 → P = (P×R×10)÷100 → R = 100÷10 = 10%.
28. **C) ₹250** — Total parts = 3+4+5 = 12; 1 part = 600÷12 = 50; largest share = 5×50 = ₹250.
29. **A) 9/20** — 0.45 = 45/100, which simplifies to 9/20.
30. **C) 17 kg 500 g** — 7300 g + 5650 g + 4550 g = 17,500 g = 17 kg 500 g.
31. **C) 19,800** — 5.5 hours × 3600 seconds/hour = 19,800 seconds.
32. **C) 114 cm** — An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides: 3 × 38 = 114 cm.
33. **C) 1,980 sq cm** — Area = 55 × 36 = 1,980 sq cm.
34. **B) 4** — A triangular pyramid (tetrahedron) has 4 triangular faces.
35. **D) 27** — Number of diagonals = n(n−3)/2 = 9×6/2 = 27.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

36. **C) ₹45,000** — 3,15,000 ÷ 7 = 45,000.
37. **C) 2,520** — Rate = 3,600 ÷ 10 = 360 bottles/hour; in 7 hours = 360 × 7 = 2,520.
38. **C) 30 min** — 6,000 ÷ 200 = 30 minutes.
39. **C) 4 h 50 min** — Total time 6:45 a.m. to 12:00 noon = 5 h 15 min; subtracting the 25-minute stop gives 4 h 50 min of actual travel.
40. **C) 2 L** — 6500 mL + 3500 mL = 10,000 mL; 10,000 ÷ 5 = 2,000 mL = 2 L per bottle.
41. **C) ₹230** — 6 × ₹42 = ₹252; ₹252 − ₹22 discount = ₹230.
42. **B) 1,050 L** — 1/6 × 2,100 = 350 L used per day; over 3 days = 1,050 L used; 2,100 − 1,050 = 1,050 L remaining.
43. **C) 72 kg** — 23400 g + 19600 g + 16250 g + 12750 g = 72,000 g = 72 kg.
44. **C) 56** — 2,352 ÷ 42 = 56 rows.
45. **C) ₹510** — Sold for 85 × ₹46 = ₹3,910; profit = ₹3,910 − ₹3,400 = ₹510.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (3 marks each)

46. **C) 22** — Working backwards: (144 + 32) ÷ 8 = 176 ÷ 8 = 22.
47. **C) 40** — Let the first number be *x*. Then second = 2*x*, third = 2*x*+25. Sum: *x* + 2*x* + (2*x*+25) = 225 → 5*x* + 25 = 225 → *x* = 40.
48. **C) ₹2,250** — After spending 1/3 on books, 2/3 of the money remains. After spending 1/5 of that on stationery, 4/5 of 2/3 = 8/15 of the original remains. So 8/15 of the total = ₹1,200 → total = ₹2,250.
49. **C) 239** — Girls = (2 × 115) + 9 = 239. Check: 115 + 239 = 354. ✓
50. **C) 47** — Each term is the sum of the previous two: 18 + 29 = 47.

**Scoring guide** (out of 60, Level‑2-difficulty paper): 32+ is a very
strong result at this difficulty. 22–31 is solid. Below 22 — review *why*
each missed question was harder (an extra step, a bigger number, two ideas
combined) rather than the raw score.
