# Week 4 — Fractions & Decimals — Answer Key

**Q1. A) 10/12** — Multiply top and bottom of 5/6 by 2: 10/12.

**Q2. B) 3/7 < 4/7** — Same denominator, so compare numerators: 3 < 4.

**Q3. C) 5/6** — As decimals: 2/3≈0.67, 3/5=0.6, 5/6≈0.83, 1/2=0.5 — the greatest is 5/6.

**Q4. A) 2/3** — The greatest common factor of 24 and 36 is 12; 24÷12=2, 36÷12=3.

**Q5. A) 7/9** — Same denominator: add the numerators: 2+4+1=7, so 7/9.

**Q6. A) 1/2** — 7/8 − 3/8 = 4/8, which simplifies to 1/2.

**Q7. A) 1/2** — (2×3)/(3×4) = 6/12 = 1/2.

**Q8. A) 3/2** — Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal: 3/4 × 2/1 = 6/4 = 3/2.

**Q9. A) 3/5** — 0.6 = 6/10, which simplifies to 3/5.

**Q10. A) 0.35** — 7/20 = 35/100 = 0.35.

**Q11. A) 6.12** — 2.45 + 3.67 = 6.12.

**Q12. A) 5.65** — 10 − 4.35 = 5.65.

**Q13. A) 12.8** — 3.2 × 4 = 12.8.

**Q14. A) 2.75 m** — 5.5 − 2.75 = 2.75.

**Q15. A) 0.5, 0.55, 3/5, 2/3** — Converting all to decimals: 0.5, 0.55, 0.6 (3/5), 0.667 (2/3) — already in ascending order.
