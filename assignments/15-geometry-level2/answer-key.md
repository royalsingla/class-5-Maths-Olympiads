# Geometry, Perimeter & Area — Level 2 — Answer Key

1. **C) 276 sq m** — Outer area = 30×22 = 660 sq m. A path 3 m wide running just inside the boundary leaves an inner rectangle of (30−6)×(22−6) = 24×16 = 384 sq m. Path area = 660−384 = 276 sq m.
2. **C) 444 sq m** — A path built outside adds 3 m all around, so the outer rectangle becomes (40+6)×(28+6) = 46×34 = 1,564 sq m. Inner (the park itself) = 40×28 = 1,120 sq m. Path area = 1,564−1,120 = 444 sq m.
3. **C) 616 sq cm** — Outer area = 50×35 = 1,750 sq cm. The photo itself (inner rectangle, 4 cm in from each side) = (50−8)×(35−8) = 42×27 = 1,134 sq cm. Frame area = 1,750−1,134 = 616 sq cm.
4. **C) 47 sq m** — Total area = 7×5 + 4×3 = 35+12 = 47 sq m (the two sections' areas simply add, regardless of exactly how they're joined).
5. **C) 110 sq cm** — Original area = 14×9 = 126 sq cm. Removing the 4×4 corner square removes 16 sq cm. Remaining area = 126−16 = 110 sq cm.
6. **B) 3 times** — Perimeter = 2(*l*+*b*). Making both sides 3 times as long: new perimeter = 2(3*l*+3*b*) = 3 × 2(*l*+*b*) = 3× the original perimeter.
7. **D) 16 times** — Area = side². Making the side 4 times as long: new area = (4×side)² = 16×side² = 16× the original area.
8. **C) 70 cm** — Six 5 cm squares in a row form a rectangle 30 cm long (6×5) and 5 cm wide. Perimeter = 2(30+5) = 70 cm.
9. **C) 192 sq cm** — Each tile has area 6×4 = 24 sq cm. Eight tiles placed with no gaps or overlaps always cover a total area of 8×24 = 192 sq cm, however they're arranged.
10. **C) 1,536 sq m** — Field area = 50×32 = 1,600 sq m. Square plot area = 8×8 = 64 sq m. Uncovered area = 1,600−64 = 1,536 sq m.
11. **C) 46 cm** — The original rectangle's perimeter is 2(14+9) = 46 cm. Cutting a rectangular notch out of a corner removes two short stretches of the original boundary but adds two new cut edges of exactly the same total length back in — so the perimeter of the L-shape is unchanged: still 46 cm.
12. **B) 2** — The combined rectangle has a line of symmetry along the join between the two squares, and another line of symmetry running the other way through its centre — 2 in total.
13. **C) 75°** — The angles of a quadrilateral always add up to 360°. Fourth angle = 360 − 80 − 95 − 110 = 75°.
14. **C) 42** — The full grid has 8×6 = 48 unit squares. Removing a 3×2 block removes 6 unit squares. Remaining area = 48−6 = 42 square units.
15. **C) 48 cm** — Area 64 sq cm means each tile has side 8 cm (since 8×8 = 64). Two tiles side by side form a rectangle 16 cm by 8 cm. Perimeter = 2(16+8) = 48 cm.

---

**How to use this:** 10+/15 is strong for a Level-2-focused set. Q11 (the
corner-notch perimeter) is the trickiest idea here — it's worth re-deriving
with pencil and paper: sketch the rectangle, cut the corner, and measure
each remaining side yourself rather than just trusting the shortcut.
