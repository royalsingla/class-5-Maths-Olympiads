# Logical Reasoning — Level 2 (Compound Puzzles)

A dedicated harder follow-on to Weeks 9–10, for students who've qualified
Level 1. Every question here stacks two or more rules or clues together —
Level 1 tests one reasoning skill at a time, Level 2 combines them. 15
questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Six friends — Ishaan, Jaya, Kabir, Lata, Mohan, Nisha — sit in a row facing north. Jaya sits at the left end. Kabir sits third from the left. Lata sits immediately to the right of Kabir. Nisha sits at the right end. Mohan sits between Lata and Nisha. Find the seating order, left to right.
A) Jaya, Ishaan, Kabir, Lata, Mohan, Nisha
B) Jaya, Kabir, Ishaan, Lata, Mohan, Nisha
C) Ishaan, Jaya, Kabir, Lata, Mohan, Nisha
D) Jaya, Ishaan, Kabir, Mohan, Lata, Nisha

**Q2.** A word is coded by first moving each letter 3 steps forward in the alphabet, then reversing the order of the resulting letters. Using this rule, DOG becomes JRG. What does LAMP become?
A) SPDO  B) SPOD  C) RPDO  D) SQDO

**Q3.** Vikram is Anita's husband. Anita is Rohit's mother. How is Vikram related to Rohit?
A) Father  B) Uncle  C) Grandfather  D) Brother

**Q4.** In a competition, Karan scored more than Leena but less than Mira. Nitin scored less than Leena. Who scored the second-highest?
A) Karan  B) Leena  C) Mira  D) Nitin

**Q5.** Which word does NOT belong with the others?
A) LISTEN  B) SILENT  C) ENLIST  D) LINEN

**Q6.** In a code, each letter is replaced by its position number in the alphabet, and then 4 is added to each number (so C, the 3rd letter, becomes 3+4 = 7). What is the code for the word SUN?
A) 23-25-18  B) 23-24-18  C) 22-25-18  D) 23-25-17

**Q7.** 20th June is a Friday. What day of the week is 15th July of the same year?
A) Monday  B) Tuesday  C) Wednesday  D) Sunday

**Q8.** Find the next number: 4, 9, 19, 39, 79, ___
A) 149  B) 154  C) 159  D) 164

**Q9.** Three friends — Dev, Isha, Farah — each play a different sport (Chess, Tennis, Badminton) and live in a different town (Pune, Nashik, Nagpur). Dev does not play Chess. The person who plays Tennis lives in Nashik. Isha plays Badminton. Farah does not live in Nagpur. Which sport does Farah play, and in which town does she live?
A) Chess, Pune  B) Tennis, Nashik  C) Chess, Nagpur  D) Badminton, Pune

**Q10.** Meera's father is Arjun. Arjun's father is Deepak. How is Deepak related to Meera?
A) Father  B) Grandfather  C) Uncle  D) Brother

**Q11.** Tara starts at her home facing North and walks 10 m. She turns right and walks 6 m. She turns right again and walks 10 m. How far is she from her home now, and in which direction?
A) 6 m East  B) 6 m West  C) 16 m East  D) 4 m East

**Q12.** Statements: All squares are rectangles. All rectangles are quadrilaterals. Conclusions: I. All squares are quadrilaterals. II. Some quadrilaterals are squares. Which conclusion(s) logically follow?
A) Only I follows  B) Only II follows  C) Both I and II follow  D) Neither follows

**Q13.** Arrange the following in a logical (life-cycle) order: 1. Seed  2. Flower  3. Plant  4. Fruit  5. Tree
A) 1, 3, 5, 2, 4  B) 1, 3, 2, 5, 4  C) 1, 5, 3, 2, 4  D) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5

**Q14.** If + means ×, × means −, − means ÷, and ÷ means +, find the value of: 20 − 4 + 3 × 2 ÷ 1
A) 12  B) 13  C) 14  D) 15

**Q15.** 6 is to 36 as 8 is to ___.
A) 48  B) 56  C) 64  D) 72
