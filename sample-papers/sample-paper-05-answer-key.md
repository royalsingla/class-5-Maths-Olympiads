# Sample Paper 5 — Answer Key

Total: 60 marks (45 × 1 mark + 5 × 3 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **D) Peacock** — Lion, Tiger, and Leopard are big cats; Peacock is a bird.
2. **B) Speed** — A thermometer measures temperature; a speedometer measures speed.
3. **A) GHVN** — The rule is "+3 letters": D→G, E→H, S→V, K→N.
4. **C) 176** — Each term doubles: 11, 22, 44, 88, 176.
5. **B) 17th** — Position from the right = 35 − 19 + 1 = 17.
6. **C) North** — Two 90° anticlockwise turns from South = 180° turn = North.
7. **A) 2, 1, 4, 3** — A plant's growth: Seed → Seedling → Sapling → Flowering Plant.
8. **C) 53** — 33, 44, and 66 are multiples of 11; 53 is not.
9. **B) Friday** — 45 days = 6 full weeks (42 days, same day: Tuesday) + 3 more days → Wed, Thu, Fri.
10. **B) Cousin** — Suresh's father's only sister is his paternal aunt; her daughter is his cousin.
11. **C) 127** — The pattern is "double and add 1": 7×2+1=15, 15×2+1=31, 31×2+1=63, 63×2+1=127.
12. **A) EYPBCL** — The rule is "−2 letters" (A wraps to Y): G→E, A→Y, R→P, D→B, E→C, N→L.
13. **C) Rectangle** — Pyramid, Cone, and Cylinder are 3-D solids; a Rectangle is a flat (2-D) shape.
14. **C) C** — Order: C > A > B, and D is last — so C finished first.
15. **C) 38** — The gaps increase by 2 each time (+3,+5,+7,+9,+11): 3,6,11,18,27,38.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

16. **A) 74,134** — 46,789 + 27,345 = 74,134.
17. **A) 40,528** — 80,006 − 39,478 = 40,528.
18. **A) 34,068** — 5,678 × 6 = 34,068.
19. **A) 9,639** — 57,834 ÷ 6 = 9,639.
20. **C) 18** — HCF of 54 (2×3³) and 72 (2³×3²) is 2×3² = 18.
21. **A) 42** — Multiples of 14: 14,28,42... Multiples of 21: 21,42... First common one = 42.
22. **A) 4/18** — Multiply top and bottom of 2/9 by 2.
23. **A) 10/11** — Same denominator: 6+3+1=10, so 10/11.
24. **A) 16/25** — 0.64 = 64/100, which simplifies (÷4) to 16/25.
25. **C) 121** — 55% of 220 = 0.55 × 220 = 121.
26. **C) 52%** — 13/25 = 0.52 = 52%.
27. **C) 44** — 1 part = 16 ÷ 4 = 4; larger = 11 parts = 44.
28. **B) 20%** — Loss = 850−680 = ₹170; loss % = 170/850 × 100 = 20%.
29. **C) ₹1,800** — SI = (9000×5×4)÷100 = ₹1,800.
30. **A) 7,008** — 7 kg = 7,000 g; + 8 g = 7,008 g.
31. **C) 195** — 3.25 × 60 = 195 minutes.
32. **B) 130 cm** — Perimeter = 2×(42+23) = 130 cm.
33. **B) 289 sq cm** — Area = 17×17 = 289 sq cm.
34. **C) 4** — A square has 4 lines of symmetry.
35. **B) 3** — Three-quarters of 360° = 270°; 270÷90 = 3.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

36. **C) 54,400** — 3,400 × 16 = 54,400.
37. **B) ₹2,275** — 6400 − 1950 − 2175 = 2,275.
38. **C) 450 L** — 4/9 of 810 = 360 L used; 810 − 360 = 450 L left.
39. **C) ₹45** — 1,215 ÷ 27 = 45.
40. **B) 323** — 17 × 19 = 323.
41. **C) ₹2,100** — SI = (6000×7×5)÷100 = ₹2,100.
42. **B) 18%** — Profit = 3304−2800 = ₹504; profit % = 504/2800 × 100 = 18%.
43. **C) 84 km** — 1 h 20 min = 4/3 hours; 63 × 4/3 = 84 km.
44. **B) 6 cups** — 3/10 × 20 = 6 cups.
45. **C) 140** — 40% of 350 = 0.40 × 350 = 140.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (3 marks each)

46. **C) 300** — If the original number is *x*: 1.45*x* = 435 → *x* = 300.
47. **C) 30** — Five consecutive numbers *n*−2 through *n*+2 sum to 5*n* = 150 → *n* = 30 (the middle number).
48. **B) ₹7,000** — SI = (P×3×8)÷100 = 0.24×P = 1,680 → P = 1,680÷0.24 = ₹7,000.
49. **C) ₹3,000** — After snacks, 5/6 of the money remains. After books (2/5 of that spent), 3/5 of 5/6 = 1/2 of the original remains. So 1/2 of the total = ₹1,500 → total = ₹3,000.
50. **C) 20** — Let the numbers be 3*x* and 4*x*. After decreasing each by 5: (3*x*−5)/(4*x*−5) = 2/3 → 3(3*x*−5) = 2(4*x*−5) → 9*x*−15 = 8*x*−10 → *x* = 5. Larger number = 4×5 = 20.
