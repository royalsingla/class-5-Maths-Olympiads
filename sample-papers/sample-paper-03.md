# Sample Paper 3 — Class 5 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 50 questions, 60 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. Set a timer before you start.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Hammer  B) Saw  C) Nail  D) Shirt

**Q2.** Pilot is to Airplane as Captain is to ___.
A) Ship  B) Car  C) Train  D) Bicycle

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 4 steps forward in the alphabet, LIME becomes PMQI. Using the same rule, how is ROSE written?
A) VSWI  B) VSWH  C) USWI  D) VTWI

**Q4.** Complete the series: 7, 14, 28, 56, ___
A) 98  B) 104  C) 112  D) 120

**Q5.** In a row of 32 students, Simran is 14th from the right. What is her position from the left?
A) 18th  B) 19th  C) 20th  D) 17th

**Q6.** Facing East, Priya turns 90° anticlockwise, then another 90° anticlockwise. Which direction does she face now?
A) North  B) South  C) West  D) East

**Q7.** Arrange in a logical order of education level: 1. University  2. Primary School  3. High School  4. Kindergarten
A) 4, 2, 3, 1  B) 1, 2, 3, 4  C) 4, 3, 2, 1  D) 2, 4, 3, 1

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 12  B) 18  C) 23  D) 30

**Q9.** If today is Friday, what day will it be after 31 days?
A) Sunday  B) Monday  C) Tuesday  D) Saturday

**Q10.** Sonia introduced a boy as the son of her father's brother. How is the boy related to Sonia?
A) Brother  B) Cousin  C) Nephew  D) Uncle

**Q11.** Find the missing number: 4, 9, 19, 39, 79, ___
A) 149  B) 154  C) 159  D) 164

**Q12.** In a certain code, WATER is written as YCVGT (each letter moved 2 steps forward). How is MONEY written in the same code?
A) OQPGA  B) OQPGZ  C) OPPGA  D) OQOGA

**Q13.** Find the odd one out:
A) Cube  B) Cuboid  C) Square  D) Pyramid

**Q14.** Neha scored more than Kabir. Kabir scored more than Meera. Arjun scored less than Meera. Who scored the lowest?
A) Neha  B) Kabir  C) Meera  D) Arjun

**Q15.** Complete the series: 1, 8, 27, 64, ___
A) 100  B) 110  C) 125  D) 150

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35, 1 mark each)

**Q16.** 67,845 + 28,956 = ?
A) 96,801  B) 96,701  C) 95,801  D) 96,901

**Q17.** 70,002 − 38,459 = ?
A) 31,543  B) 31,443  C) 31,643  D) 32,543

**Q18.** 6,789 × 7 = ?
A) 47,523  B) 47,423  C) 47,623  D) 46,523

**Q19.** 75,348 ÷ 6 = ?
A) 12,558  B) 12,548  C) 12,568  D) 12,658

**Q20.** Find the HCF of 18 and 27.
A) 6  B) 9  C) 3  D) 18

**Q21.** Find the LCM of 8 and 10.
A) 18  B) 40  C) 80  D) 20

**Q22.** Which fraction is equivalent to 5/8?
A) 10/16  B) 8/5  C) 5/16  D) 10/8

**Q23.** 5/12 + 3/12 + 2/12 = ?
A) 5/6  B) 10/144  C) 5/12  D) 1

**Q24.** Convert 0.28 to a fraction in simplest form.
A) 7/25  B) 7/20  C) 2/7  D) 14/25

**Q25.** What is 65% of 180?
A) 107  B) 112  C) 117  D) 122

**Q26.** Express 9/20 as a percentage.
A) 40%  B) 45%  C) 50%  D) 55%

**Q27.** Two numbers are in the ratio 5:8. If the larger is 40, find the smaller.
A) 20  B) 22  C) 25  D) 28

**Q28.** The cost price of an item is ₹540 and the selling price is ₹486. Find the loss percentage.
A) 8%  B) 10%  C) 12%  D) 15%

**Q29.** Find the simple interest on ₹6,000 at 9% per annum for 2 years.
A) ₹1,000  B) ₹1,040  C) ₹1,080  D) ₹1,100

**Q30.** Convert 3 L 120 mL into millilitres.
A) 3,120  B) 3,012  C) 3,102  D) 31,200

**Q31.** Convert 195 minutes into hours and minutes.
A) 3 h 5 min  B) 3 h 15 min  C) 3 h 25 min  D) 3 h 35 min

**Q32.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 27 cm and breadth 13 cm.
A) 40 cm  B) 80 cm  C) 160 cm  D) 351 cm

**Q33.** Find the area of a square with side 11 cm.
A) 111 sq cm  B) 121 sq cm  C) 44 sq cm  D) 131 sq cm

**Q34.** How many lines of symmetry does a regular hexagon have?
A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 8

**Q35.** How many right angles make one and a half full turns?
A) 4  B) 5  C) 6  D) 8

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45, 1 mark each)

**Q36.** A factory fills 1,850 bottles each day. How many bottles does it fill in 18 days?
A) 31,300  B) 33,300  C) 35,300  D) 32,300

**Q37.** Kavya had ₹9,500. She spent ₹3,200 and then ₹2,150. How much money does she have left?
A) ₹4,150  B) ₹4,250  C) ₹4,050  D) ₹4,350

**Q38.** A tank holds 720 L. If 3/4 of it is used, how much water is left?
A) 160 L  B) 170 L  C) 180 L  D) 190 L

**Q39.** 24 kg of wheat costs ₹720. Find the cost of 1 kg.
A) ₹25  B) ₹28  C) ₹30  D) ₹32

**Q40.** 11 rows of chairs have 27 chairs each. How many chairs are there in total?
A) 287  B) 297  C) 307  D) 317

**Q41.** A man deposits ₹5,000 at 9% simple interest per year. How much interest will he earn in 3 years?
A) ₹1,250  B) ₹1,300  C) ₹1,350  D) ₹1,400

**Q42.** A shopkeeper bought goods for ₹3,200 and sold them for ₹2,880. Find the loss percentage.
A) 8%  B) 10%  C) 12%  D) 15%

**Q43.** A car travels at 80 km/h. How far will it travel in 2 hours 45 minutes?
A) 200 km  B) 210 km  C) 220 km  D) 230 km

**Q44.** A recipe needs 5/6 cup of milk. How much milk is needed for 12 such recipes?
A) 8 cups  B) 9 cups  C) 10 cups  D) 11 cups

**Q45.** In a survey of 320 students, 55% like artwork. How many students like artwork?
A) 166  B) 171  C) 176  D) 181

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q46–Q50, 3 marks each)

**Q46.** A number is increased by 30% to get 390. Find the original number.
A) 280  B) 290  C) 300  D) 310

**Q47.** The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is 99. Find the middle number.
A) 31  B) 33  C) 35  D) 37

**Q48.** The simple interest on a sum for 5 years at 6% per annum is ₹2,400. Find the sum.
A) ₹7,000  B) ₹7,500  C) ₹8,000  D) ₹8,500

**Q49.** Raj had some money. He spent 2/5 of it on rent and 1/3 of the remainder on groceries. He is left with ₹1,200. How much money did he have at first?
A) ₹2,500  B) ₹2,800  C) ₹3,000  D) ₹3,200

**Q50.** The ratio of the ages of two cousins is 4:7. After 6 years, the ratio becomes 5:8. Find the younger cousin's present age.
A) 20  B) 22  C) 24  D) 26
