# Week 6 — Measurement & Money

Length, weight, capacity, time, temperature, and money. 15 questions,
4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Convert 7.5 km into metres.
A) 750 m  B) 7,500 m  C) 75,000 m  D) 7.5 m

**Q2.** Convert 3,450 g into kg and g.
A) 3 kg 450 g  B) 34 kg 50 g  C) 3 kg 45 g  D) 345 kg

**Q3.** Convert 2.5 L into mL.
A) 250 mL  B) 2,500 mL  C) 25,000 mL  D) 2.5 mL

**Q4.** 5 km 600 m + 3 km 750 m = ?
A) 8 km 350 m  B) 9 km 350 m  C) 9 km 450 m  D) 8 km 950 m

**Q5.** 12 kg − 4 kg 650 g = ?
A) 7 kg 350 g  B) 7 kg 650 g  C) 8 kg 350 g  D) 7 kg 250 g

**Q6.** Convert 150 minutes into hours and minutes.
A) 2 h 10 min  B) 2 h 30 min  C) 2 h 50 min  D) 3 h

**Q7.** Which of these temperatures is the coldest?
A) 15°C  B) 25°C  C) 5°C  D) 35°C

**Q8.** Aman bought 3 notebooks at ₹45 each, 2 pens at ₹12 each, and a geometry box for ₹85. Find the total amount spent.
A) ₹234  B) ₹244  C) ₹254  D) ₹264

**Q9.** A jug holds 1.8 L of juice. How many 300 mL glasses can be filled completely?
A) 5  B) 6  C) 7  D) 8

**Q10.** A tank contains 850 L of water. 275 L is used in the morning and 180 L in the evening. How much water is left?
A) 385 L  B) 395 L  C) 405 L  D) 415 L

**Q11.** Reema's school day starts at 8:20 a.m. and ends at 2:50 p.m. How long is the school day?
A) 6 h 10 min  B) 6 h 20 min  C) 6 h 30 min  D) 6 h 40 min

**Q12.** 5 identical boxes weigh 12.5 kg together. Find the weight of 1 box.
A) 2 kg  B) 2.5 kg  C) 3 kg  D) 3.5 kg

**Q13.** A shopkeeper has 15 kg 250 g of sugar. He sells 9 kg 400 g. How much sugar is left?
A) 5 kg 750 g  B) 5 kg 850 g  C) 6 kg 850 g  D) 5 kg 950 g

**Q14.** A cinema show starts at 6:45 p.m. and lasts 2 hours 35 minutes. At what time does it end?
A) 9:10 p.m.  B) 9:20 p.m.  C) 9:30 p.m.  D) 9:15 p.m.

**Q15.** A milkman has 40 L of milk. He sells it in packets of 500 mL, at ₹25 per packet. Find the total amount he earns from selling all the milk.
A) ₹1,800  B) ₹2,000  C) ₹2,200  D) ₹2,500
