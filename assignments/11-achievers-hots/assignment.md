# Week 11 — Achievers / HOTS Mixed Practice

2-and-3-step problems pulling from every topic covered so far — including
percentage, ratio, and profit/loss, which Class 4 didn't have. 15
questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed. This is the hardest assignment in the
plan — don't worry if it takes longer or needs a second attempt.

---

**Q1.** A number, when increased by 15%, becomes 345. Find the original number.
A) 280  B) 290  C) 300  D) 310

**Q2.** The sum of four consecutive odd numbers is 96. Find the smallest number.
A) 19  B) 21  C) 23  D) 25

**Q3.** A rectangular field is 36 m long and 24 m wide. Find the cost of fencing it at ₹45 per metre.
A) ₹5,200  B) ₹5,400  C) ₹5,600  D) ₹5,800

**Q4.** Rohan had some money. He spent 2/5 of it on books and ₹240 on a bag. He is left with ₹360. How much money did he have at first?
A) ₹900  B) ₹1,000  C) ₹1,100  D) ₹1,200

**Q5.** A sum of ₹12,000 is lent at 10% simple interest per annum. After how many years will the interest equal half the principal?
A) 4 years  B) 5 years  C) 6 years  D) 8 years

**Q6.** Find the next number in the pattern: 1, 3, 7, 15, 31, ___
A) 55  B) 59  C) 63  D) 67

**Q7.** A number leaves a remainder of 7 when divided by 12, and the quotient is 23. Find the number.
A) 276  B) 279  C) 283  D) 289

**Q8.** In a school, the ratio of teachers to students is 1:18. If there are 36 teachers, how many students are there?
A) 588  B) 612  C) 648  D) 684

**Q9.** A shopkeeper allows a 10% discount on the marked price and still makes a 20% profit on an item whose cost price is ₹450. Find the marked price.
A) ₹550  B) ₹575  C) ₹600  D) ₹625

**Q10.** Four friends share ₹2,400 such that each gets ₹150 more than the previous one, with the first getting the least. Find the amount the fourth friend gets.
A) ₹750  B) ₹800  C) ₹825  D) ₹900

**Q11.** A square and a rectangle have equal perimeters. If the square's side is 18 cm and the rectangle's length is 24 cm, find the rectangle's breadth.
A) 10 cm  B) 12 cm  C) 14 cm  D) 16 cm

**Q12.** Rani is standing in a queue with 14 people ahead of her and 9 people behind her. How many people are in the queue in total, including Rani?
A) 22  B) 23  C) 24  D) 25

**Q13.** Two pipes fill a tank at the rate of 450 L/hour and 300 L/hour respectively. If both run together for 4 hours, how much water fills the tank?
A) 2,800 L  B) 2,900 L  C) 3,000 L  D) 3,100 L

**Q14.** The sum of two numbers is 1,450. If the larger number is 850, find the ratio of the smaller number to the larger, in simplest form.
A) 6:17  B) 12:17  C) 12:34  D) 6:8.5

**Q15.** The digits of a 2-digit number differ by 3, and their sum is 11. If the tens digit is the larger one, find the number.
A) 74  B) 83  C) 47  D) 29
