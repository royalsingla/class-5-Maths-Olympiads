# Week 3 — Factors, Multiples, HCF/LCM & Number Patterns — Answer Key

**Q1. B) 7** — 84 = 7 × 12, so 7 is a factor. (5, 9, 11 don't divide 84 exactly.)

**Q2. A) 39** — 39 = 13 × 3. (42, 45, 48 are not multiples of 13.)

**Q3. C) 12** — 48 = 2⁴×3; 60 = 2²×3×5. Common factors: 2²×3 = 12.

**Q4. B) 36** — Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36... Multiples of 18: 18, 36... First common one = 36.

**Q5. C) 59** — 59 has no factors besides 1 and itself. (51=3×17, 57=3×19, 63=9×7.)

**Q6. C) 9** — Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36 — that's 9 factors.

**Q7. B) 36** — LCM of 4 and 9 (they share no common factor) = 4 × 9 = 36.

**Q8. D) 115** — Multiples of 11: ...77, 88, 99, 110, 121... 115 isn't among them.

**Q9. C) 79** — Each term is "double, then add 1": 4×2+1=9, 9×2+1=19, 19×2+1=39, 39×2+1=79.

**Q10. C) 42** — These follow n×(n+1): 1×2=2, 2×3=6, 3×4=12, 4×5=20, 5×6=30, 6×7=42.

**Q11. C) 49** — Square numbers: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5², 6², 7² = 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49.

**Q12. C) 18** — 36=2²×3², 54=2×3³, 90=2×3²×5. Common factors: 2×3² = 18.

**Q13. C) 8** — 96 ÷ 12 = 8 baskets.

**Q14. B) 10:12 a.m.** — They ring together again after LCM(18, 24) = 72 minutes = 1 h 12 min; 9:00 a.m. + 1 h 12 min = 10:12 a.m.

**Q15. A) 15** — 15 is a factor of 60 (60÷15=4), a multiple of 5, and greater than 10. (8 isn't even a multiple of 5; 14 and 25 aren't factors of 60.)
