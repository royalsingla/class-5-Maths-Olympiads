# Week 9 — Reasoning: Patterns, Analogy, Coding & Word Puzzles — Answer Key

**Q1. D) Chair** — Guitar, Violin, and Tabla are musical instruments; a Chair is furniture.

**Q2. B) Student** — A doctor treats a patient; a teacher teaches a student.

**Q3. C) 30** — 16, 25, and 49 are perfect squares (4², 5², 7²); 30 is not.

**Q4. A) GRJ** — The rule is "+3 letters": D→G, O→R, G→J.

**Q5. B) J** — The pattern skips one letter each time: B, D, F, H, J.

**Q6. A) 1, 3, 2, 4** — Alphabetically: Mango, Market, Monkey, Moon (comparing letter by letter: "Man..." < "Mar..." < "Monk..." < "Moon" — the 3rd letter decides Monkey vs. Moon: 'n' comes before 'o').

**Q7. D) Sphere** — Triangle, Square, and Pentagon are flat (2-D) shapes; a Sphere is a solid (3-D) shape.

**Q8. B) Cut** — A pen is used to write; scissors are used to cut.

**Q9. A) ICTFGP** — The rule is "+2 letters": G→I, A→C, R→T, D→F, E→G, N→P.

**Q10. C) 111** — Each term is "double, then add 1": 6×2+1=13, 13×2+1=27, 27×2+1=55, 55×2+1=111.

**Q11. C) Hen** — Cow, Goat, and Sheep are mammals; a Hen is a bird.

**Q12. C) Meena** — The order is Priya, Simran, Raj, Meena, Tina — Meena is the only person left to fill the middle seat, between Raj and Tina.

**Q13. D) Rainbow** — Red, Blue, and Green are each a single colour; a Rainbow is a mix of many colours.

**Q14. B) P** — The pattern skips one letter backward each time: Z, X, V, T, R, P.

**Q15. B) 38** — 12 C 3 means 12 × 3 = 36; 8 D 4 means 8 ÷ 4 = 2; 36 A 2 means 36 + 2 = 38.
