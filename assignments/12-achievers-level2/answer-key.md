# Achievers — Level 2 — Answer Key

1. **C) 225** — Original = 360 ÷ 1.20 = 300. Decreasing 300 by 25%: 300 × 0.75 = 225.
2. **D) 125** — Total parts = 2+3+5 = 10. 1 part = 250÷10 = 25. Largest = 5×25 = 125.
3. **C) ₹4,200** — After spending 2/7 on rent, 5/7 of the money remains. After spending 15% of that on groceries, 85% of 5/7 = 17/28 of the original remains. So 17/28 of the total = ₹2,550 → total = ₹4,200.
4. **C) 10** — Let the daughter's age be *x*; mother's age = 3*x*. After 10 years: 3*x*+10 = 2(*x*+10) → 3*x*+10 = 2*x*+20 → *x* = 10.
5. **B) 6 hours** — Pipe A fills 1/10 of the tank per hour, Pipe B fills 1/15 per hour. Together: 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6 of the tank per hour, so the tank fills in 6 hours.
6. **C) 33** — Let the numbers be *x*, *x*+2, *x*+4, *x*+6. Sum: 4*x*+12 = 120 → *x* = 27. Largest = 27+6 = 33.
7. **C) 80** — Larger number = (sum + difference) ÷ 2 = (134+26) ÷ 2 = 80.
8. **C) 12** — Let *a* = number of ₹10 coins, *b* = number of ₹5 coins. *a*+*b* = 22 and 10*a*+5*b* = 170. Substituting *b* = 22−*a*: 10*a* + 5(22−*a*) = 170 → 5*a* + 110 = 170 → *a* = 12.
9. **C) 7 h 30 min** — Speed = 300 ÷ 5 = 60 km/h. Time for 450 km = 450 ÷ 60 = 7.5 hours = 7 h 30 min.
10. **B) 24** — Let the tens digit be *t*; the ones digit is *t*+2. The number = 11*t*+2, and the digit sum = 2*t*+2. Setting 11*t*+2 = 4(2*t*+2) gives 11*t*+2 = 8*t*+8 → *t* = 2. Number = 11×2+2 = 24. Check: digit sum = 2+4 = 6, and 4×6 = 24. ✓
11. **C) 425 sq cm** — Perimeter = 2(*l*+*b*) = 84 → *l*+*b* = 42. Since *l* = *b*+8: 2*b*+8 = 42 → *b* = 17, *l* = 25. Area = 17×25 = 425 sq cm.
12. **D) 158** — Each term is double the previous term plus 2: 38×2+2 = 78, 78×2+2 = 158.
13. **C) ₹1,710** — After a 10% discount: ₹2,000 × 0.90 = ₹1,800. After a further 5% discount: ₹1,800 × 0.95 = ₹1,710.
14. **C) ₹5,000** — The interest earned between year 4 and year 6 is ₹8,000−₹7,000 = ₹1,000 over 2 years, so ₹500 per year. Principal = amount at year 4 minus 4 years' interest = ₹7,000 − (4×₹500) = ₹5,000.
15. **C) 123** — The number must leave remainder 4 when divided by 7 and remainder 3 when divided by 5. Checking numbers between 100 and 150 that fit both conditions gives 123 (123 ÷ 7 = 17 remainder 4; 123 ÷ 5 = 24 remainder 3).

---

**How to use this:** 10+/15 is strong for a Level-2-focused set. Q1, Q13,
and Q14 are the ones most students underrate — successive-percentage-change
and two-point simple-interest problems come up constantly at Level 2, so
if any of these tripped you up, redo similar ones from Week 5 first before
retrying this set.
