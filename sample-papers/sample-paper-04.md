# Sample Paper 4 — Class 5 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 50 questions, 60 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. Set a timer before you start.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Rose  B) Lily  C) Lotus  D) Carrot

**Q2.** Key is to Lock as Password is to ___.
A) Computer  B) Account  C) Door  D) Car

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 5 steps forward in the alphabet, BAG becomes GFL. Using the same rule, how is CAP written?
A) HFU  B) HFV  C) GFU  D) HGU

**Q4.** Complete the series: 9, 18, 36, 72, ___
A) 108  B) 126  C) 144  D) 162

**Q5.** In a row of 28 students, Tarun is 16th from the left. What is his position from the right?
A) 12th  B) 13th  C) 14th  D) 11th

**Q6.** Facing West, Sahil turns 90° clockwise, then another 90° clockwise. Which direction does he face now?
A) North  B) South  C) East  D) West

**Q7.** Arrange in a logical order of a butterfly's life cycle: 1. Egg  2. Caterpillar  3. Butterfly  4. Cocoon
A) 1, 2, 4, 3  B) 1, 2, 3, 4  C) 1, 4, 2, 3  D) 2, 1, 4, 3

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 14  B) 21  C) 25  D) 35

**Q9.** If today is Sunday, what day will it be after 40 days?
A) Thursday  B) Friday  C) Saturday  D) Monday

**Q10.** Pointing to a man, Priya said, "He is the husband of my mother's sister." How is the man related to Priya?
A) Father  B) Uncle  C) Brother  D) Cousin

**Q11.** Find the missing number: 5, 11, 23, 47, ___
A) 91  B) 93  C) 95  D) 97

**Q12.** In a certain code, STONE is written as UVQPG (each letter moved 2 steps forward). How is PLANT written in the same code?
A) RNCPV  B) RNCPU  C) RMCPV  D) SNCPV

**Q13.** Find the odd one out:
A) Circle  B) Oval  C) Sphere  D) Square

**Q14.** X is heavier than Y. Z is lighter than Y. W is heavier than X. Who is the heaviest?
A) X  B) Y  C) Z  D) W

**Q15.** Complete the series: 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ___
A) 18  B) 19  C) 20  D) 21

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35, 1 mark each)

**Q16.** 78,456 + 16,789 = ?
A) 95,245  B) 95,145  C) 94,245  D) 95,345

**Q17.** 60,004 − 27,658 = ?
A) 32,346  B) 32,246  C) 32,446  D) 33,346

**Q18.** 3,789 × 8 = ?
A) 30,312  B) 30,212  C) 30,412  D) 29,312

**Q19.** 68,432 ÷ 8 = ?
A) 8,554  B) 8,544  C) 8,564  D) 8,654

**Q20.** Find the HCF of 40 and 60.
A) 10  B) 15  C) 20  D) 30

**Q21.** Find the LCM of 10 and 15.
A) 30  B) 60  C) 15  D) 150

**Q22.** Which fraction is equivalent to 7/10?
A) 14/20  B) 10/7  C) 7/20  D) 14/10

**Q23.** 4/9 + 2/9 + 1/9 = ?
A) 7/9  B) 7/27  C) 6/9  D) 1

**Q24.** Convert 0.32 to a fraction in simplest form.
A) 8/25  B) 4/25  C) 8/5  D) 16/25

**Q25.** What is 40% of 150?
A) 50  B) 55  C) 60  D) 65

**Q26.** Express 11/20 as a percentage.
A) 50%  B) 55%  C) 60%  D) 65%

**Q27.** Two numbers are in the ratio 7:9. If the smaller is 35, find the larger.
A) 40  B) 42  C) 45  D) 48

**Q28.** The cost price of an item is ₹600 and the selling price is ₹750. Find the profit percentage.
A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 30%  D) 35%

**Q29.** Find the simple interest on ₹7,000 at 4% per annum for 6 years.
A) ₹1,580  B) ₹1,620  C) ₹1,680  D) ₹1,720

**Q30.** Convert 6 kg 45 g into grams.
A) 6,045  B) 6,450  C) 6,405  D) 645

**Q31.** Convert 225 minutes into hours and minutes.
A) 3 h 25 min  B) 3 h 35 min  C) 3 h 45 min  D) 3 h 55 min

**Q32.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 31 cm and breadth 17 cm.
A) 48 cm  B) 96 cm  C) 192 cm  D) 527 cm

**Q33.** Find the area of a square with side 13 cm.
A) 156 sq cm  B) 169 sq cm  C) 26 sq cm  D) 179 sq cm

**Q34.** How many lines of symmetry does a circle have?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 4  D) Countless (infinite)

**Q35.** How many right angles make two full turns?
A) 4  B) 6  C) 8  D) 10

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45, 1 mark each)

**Q36.** A factory makes 2,750 pens each day. How many pens does it make in 40 days?
A) 1,00,000  B) 1,05,000  C) 1,10,000  D) 1,15,000

**Q37.** Neha had ₹7,200. She spent ₹2,450 and then ₹1,890. How much money does she have left?
A) ₹2,760  B) ₹2,860  C) ₹2,960  D) ₹2,660

**Q38.** A tank holds 960 L. If 5/8 of it is used, how much water is left?
A) 340 L  B) 350 L  C) 360 L  D) 370 L

**Q39.** 32 kg of rice costs ₹1,600. Find the cost of 1 kg.
A) ₹45  B) ₹48  C) ₹50  D) ₹52

**Q40.** 14 rows of chairs have 23 chairs each. How many chairs are there in total?
A) 312  B) 322  C) 332  D) 342

**Q41.** A man deposits ₹4,500 at 8% simple interest per year. How much interest will he earn in 5 years?
A) ₹1,700  B) ₹1,750  C) ₹1,800  D) ₹1,850

**Q42.** A trader bought goods for ₹2,100 and sold them for ₹2,415. Find the profit percentage.
A) 10%  B) 15%  C) 20%  D) 25%

**Q43.** A cyclist rides at 18 km/h. How far will they travel in 2 hours 50 minutes?
A) 45 km  B) 48 km  C) 51 km  D) 54 km

**Q44.** A recipe needs 7/8 cup of oil. How much oil is needed for 8 such recipes?
A) 6 cups  B) 7 cups  C) 8 cups  D) 9 cups

**Q45.** In a survey of 400 students, 42% like swimming. How many students like swimming?
A) 158  B) 163  C) 168  D) 173

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q46–Q50, 3 marks each)

**Q46.** A number is decreased by 35% to get 390. Find the original number.
A) 550  B) 580  C) 600  D) 620

**Q47.** The sum of four consecutive numbers is 106. Find the largest number.
A) 26  B) 27  C) 28  D) 29

**Q48.** The simple interest on a sum for 6 years at 5% per annum is ₹2,700. Find the sum.
A) ₹8,000  B) ₹8,500  C) ₹9,000  D) ₹9,500

**Q49.** Ananya had some money. She spent 3/8 of it on clothes and 1/5 of the remainder on books. She is left with ₹1,000. How much money did she have at first?
A) ₹1,800  B) ₹2,000  C) ₹2,200  D) ₹2,400

**Q50.** Two numbers are in the ratio 5:7. If each number is increased by 8, the new ratio becomes 3:4. Find the smaller of the two original numbers.
A) 35  B) 40  C) 45  D) 50
