# Sample Paper 1 — Class 5 Mathematics Olympiad

**Pattern:** SOF IMO Level 1 — 50 questions, 60 marks, 60 minutes, no
negative marking. Set a timer before you start (or do it untimed the
first time, per Week 11 of the practice plan).

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Square  B) Rectangle  C) Circle  D) Triangle

**Q2.** Rose is to Flower as Lion is to ___.
A) Forest  B) Animal  C) Mane  D) Roar

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved one step forward in the alphabet (A→B, B→C, …), DOG becomes EPH. Using the same rule, what does CAT become?
A) DBU  B) DCU  C) DBV  D) CBU

**Q4.** Complete the series: 4, 8, 13, 19, 26, ___
A) 32  B) 33  C) 34  D) 35

**Q5.** In a row of 20 students, Meena is 8th from the left. What is her position from the right?
A) 12th  B) 13th  C) 14th  D) 11th

**Q6.** Facing North, Rohit turns 90° clockwise, then another 90° clockwise. Which direction does he face now?
A) East  B) South  C) West  D) North

**Q7.** Arrange in a logical growth order: 1. Seed  2. Tree  3. Flower  4. Fruit  5. Sapling
A) 1, 5, 2, 3, 4  B) 1, 2, 5, 3, 4  C) 1, 5, 3, 2, 4  D) 5, 1, 2, 3, 4

**Q8.** Find the odd one out:
A) 17  B) 19  C) 21  D) 23

**Q9.** If today is Wednesday, what day will it be after 17 days?
A) Friday  B) Saturday  C) Sunday  D) Thursday

**Q10.** Pointing to a man, Sita said, "He is the son of my mother's only brother." How is the man related to Sita?
A) Brother  B) Cousin  C) Nephew  D) Uncle

**Q11.** Find the missing number: 2, 5, 11, 23, ___
A) 43  B) 45  C) 47  D) 49

**Q12.** In a certain code, MANGO is written as OCPIQ (each letter moved 2 steps forward). How is TABLE written in the same code?
A) VCDNG  B) VBDNG  C) VCDMG  D) WCDNG

**Q13.** Find the odd one out:
A) Cube  B) Sphere  C) Cone  D) Square

**Q14.** A is taller than B. C is shorter than B. D is taller than A. Who is the tallest?
A) A  B) B  C) C  D) D

**Q15.** Look at the pattern: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, ___
A) 30  B) 32  C) 36  D) 49

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35, 1 mark each)

**Q16.** 45,678 + 23,459 = ?
A) 69,137  B) 69,127  C) 68,137  D) 69,237

**Q17.** 80,005 − 34,567 = ?
A) 45,438  B) 45,338  C) 45,448  D) 46,438

**Q18.** 3,456 × 8 = ?
A) 27,648  B) 27,548  C) 27,748  D) 27,638

**Q19.** 9,576 ÷ 7 = ?
A) 1,368  B) 1,358  C) 1,378  D) 1,468

**Q20.** Find the HCF of 24 and 36.
A) 6  B) 8  C) 12  D) 18

**Q21.** Find the LCM of 6 and 8.
A) 24  B) 48  C) 12  D) 16

**Q22.** Which fraction is equivalent to 3/4?
A) 6/8  B) 4/3  C) 3/8  D) 6/9

**Q23.** 2/5 + 1/5 + 1/5 = ?
A) 4/5  B) 4/15  C) 3/5  D) 1

**Q24.** Convert 0.75 to a fraction in simplest form.
A) 3/4  B) 7/5  C) 3/5  D) 7/10

**Q25.** What is 15% of 200?
A) 20  B) 25  C) 30  D) 35

**Q26.** What is 3/4 expressed as a percentage?
A) 34%  B) 43%  C) 75%  D) 134%

**Q27.** The ratio of pens to pencils is 2:3. If there are 12 pens, how many pencils are there?
A) 15  B) 16  C) 18  D) 20

**Q28.** A shopkeeper buys a toy for ₹80 and sells it for ₹100. Find the profit percentage.
A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 30%  D) 15%

**Q29.** Find the simple interest on ₹1,000 at 5% per annum for 2 years.
A) ₹50  B) ₹100  C) ₹150  D) ₹200

**Q30.** Convert 4 kg 250 g into grams.
A) 4250 g  B) 4025 g  C) 4205 g  D) 42500 g

**Q31.** Convert 3.5 hours into minutes.
A) 180 min  B) 200 min  C) 210 min  D) 215 min

**Q32.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 14 cm and breadth 9 cm.
A) 23 cm  B) 46 cm  C) 126 cm  D) 92 cm

**Q33.** Find the area of a square with side 12 cm.
A) 48 sq cm  B) 120 sq cm  C) 144 sq cm  D) 164 sq cm

**Q34.** How many lines of symmetry does a rectangle (non-square) have?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 4  D) 0

**Q35.** How many right angles are there in a full turn around a point?
A) 2  B) 3  C) 4  D) 6

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45, 1 mark each)

**Q36.** A factory produces 2,350 bottles each day. How many bottles does it produce in 30 days?
A) 70,500  B) 69,500  C) 71,500  D) 70,050

**Q37.** Riya had ₹5,000. She spent ₹1,250 on books and ₹875 on stationery. How much money does she have left?
A) ₹2,875  B) ₹2,975  C) ₹2,785  D) ₹2,675

**Q38.** A tank holds 450 litres. If 1/3 of it is used, how much water is left?
A) 250 L  B) 300 L  C) 150 L  D) 350 L

**Q39.** A shopkeeper bought 25 kg of rice for ₹1,250. What is the cost of 1 kg of rice?
A) ₹40  B) ₹45  C) ₹50  D) ₹55

**Q40.** A school has 8 sections in Class 5, each with 42 students. How many students are there in Class 5 in total?
A) 326  B) 336  C) 346  D) 356

**Q41.** Rakesh deposited ₹2,000 in a bank at 6% simple interest per year. How much interest will he earn in 3 years?
A) ₹300  B) ₹320  C) ₹360  D) ₹400

**Q42.** A dealer bought a bicycle for ₹2,400 and sold it for ₹2,880. Find the profit percentage.
A) 15%  B) 18%  C) 20%  D) 25%

**Q43.** A train travels 60 km in 1 hour. How far will it travel in 2 hours 30 minutes at the same speed?
A) 120 km  B) 135 km  C) 150 km  D) 180 km

**Q44.** A recipe needs 3/4 cup of sugar. How much sugar is needed for 4 such recipes?
A) 2 cups  B) 3 cups  C) 4 cups  D) 3.5 cups

**Q45.** In a survey, 60% of 150 students like cricket. How many students like cricket?
A) 80  B) 85  C) 90  D) 95

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q46–Q50, 3 marks each)

**Q46.** A number is increased by 20% to get 240. Find the original number.
A) 180  B) 190  C) 200  D) 210

**Q47.** The sum of three consecutive even numbers is 72. Find the largest number.
A) 22  B) 24  C) 26  D) 28

**Q48.** A sum of money amounts to ₹1,200 as simple interest in 4 years at 5% per annum. Find the principal.
A) ₹5,000  B) ₹6,000  C) ₹7,000  D) ₹8,000

**Q49.** Meena had some money. She spent 1/4 of it on a book and 1/3 of the remainder on a pen. She is left with ₹600. How much money did she have at first?
A) ₹1,000  B) ₹1,200  C) ₹1,400  D) ₹1,500

**Q50.** The ratio of ages of A and B is 3:5. After 4 years, the ratio becomes 4:6. Find A's present age.
A) 9  B) 12  C) 15  D) 18
