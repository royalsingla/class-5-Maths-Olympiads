# Sample Paper 2 — Answer Key

Total: 60 marks (45 × 1 mark + 5 × 3 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **C) Potato** — Apple, Banana, and Mango are fruits; Potato is a vegetable.
2. **B) Sky** — A fish's home is water; a bird's natural space is the sky.
3. **A) EPH** — The rule is "+1 letter": D→E, O→P, G→H.
4. **C) 160** — Each term doubles: 5, 10, 20, 40, 80, 160.
5. **B) 17th** — Position from the right = 25 − 9 + 1 = 17.
6. **B) South** — Two 90° clockwise turns from North = South.
7. **A) 3, 1, 4, 2** — Natural growth order: Infant → Child → Teenager → Adult.
8. **C) 39** — 31, 37, 41 are prime; 39 = 3 × 13 is not.
9. **B) Saturday** — 26 days = 3 full weeks (21 days, same day: Monday) + 5 more days → Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat.
10. **B) Son** — "My father's only daughter" is Meena herself (since she has no sisters); her only son is her own son.
11. **C) 63** — The pattern is "double and add 1": 3×2+1=7, 7×2+1=15, 15×2+1=31, 31×2+1=63.
12. **A) OQWUG** — The rule is "+2 letters": M→O, O→Q, U→W, S→U, E→G.
13. **C) Triangle** — Cone, Cylinder, and Sphere are 3-D solids; a Triangle is a flat (2-D) shape.
14. **D) S** — S > P > Q > R, so S is the oldest.
15. **C) 37** — The gaps increase by 2 each time (+3,+5,+7,+9,+11): 2,5,10,17,26,37.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

16. **A) 91,356** — 56,789 + 34,567 = 91,356.
17. **A) 44,325** — 90,003 − 45,678 = 44,325.
18. **A) 41,103** — 4,567 × 9 = 41,103.
19. **A) 10,864** — 86,912 ÷ 8 = 10,864.
20. **C) 16** — HCF of 32 (2⁵) and 48 (2⁴×3) is 2⁴ = 16.
21. **A) 36** — Multiples of 9: 9,18,27,36... Multiples of 12: 12,24,36... First common one = 36.
22. **A) 8/10** — Multiply top and bottom of 4/5 by 2.
23. **A) 3/4** — Same denominator: 3+2+1=6, so 6/8, which simplifies to 3/4.
24. **A) 9/20** — 0.45 = 45/100, which simplifies (÷5) to 9/20.
25. **C) 84** — 35% of 240 = 0.35 × 240 = 84.
26. **B) 70%** — 7/10 = 0.7 = 70%.
27. **C) 30** — 1 part = 18 ÷ 3 = 6; larger = 5 parts = 30.
28. **B) 25%** — Profit = 400−320 = ₹80; profit % = 80/320 × 100 = 25%.
29. **C) ₹1,200** — SI = (4000×6×5)÷100 = ₹1,200.
30. **A) 5,080 g** — 5 kg = 5,000 g; + 80 g = 5,080 g.
31. **C) 135 min** — 2×60 + 15 = 135 minutes.
32. **B) 60 cm** — Perimeter = 2×(19+11) = 60 cm.
33. **B) 196 sq cm** — Area = 14×14 = 196 sq cm.
34. **B) 1** — A (non-equilateral) isosceles triangle has exactly 1 line of symmetry.
35. **B) 3** — Three-quarters of 360° = 270°; 270÷90 = 3.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

36. **B) 80,000** — 3,200 × 25 = 80,000.
37. **A) ₹3,775** — 8000 − 2350 − 1875 = 3,775.
38. **B) 180 L** — 2/3 of 540 = 360 L used; 540 − 360 = 180 L left.
39. **C) ₹50** — 900 ÷ 18 = 50.
40. **B) 324** — 9 × 36 = 324.
41. **C) ₹840** — SI = (3000×7×4)÷100 = ₹840.
42. **B) 15%** — Profit = 2070−1800 = ₹270; profit % = 270/1800 × 100 = 15%.
43. **C) 125 km** — 1 h 40 min = 5/3 hours; 75 × 5/3 = 125 km.
44. **B) 4 cups** — 2/3 × 6 = 4 cups.
45. **C) 108** — 45% of 240 = 0.45 × 240 = 108.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (3 marks each)

46. **C) 300** — If the original number is *x*: 0.75*x* = 225 → *x* = 300.
47. **B) 35** — Three consecutive multiples of 5: *n*, *n*+5, *n*+10, summing to 3*n*+15 = 90 → *n* = 25; the largest is 25+10 = 35.
48. **C) ₹10,000** — SI = (P×8×4)÷100 = 0.32×P = 3,200 → P = 3,200÷0.32 = ₹10,000.
49. **C) ₹1,800** — After the dress, 2/3 of the money remains. After shoes (1/4 of that spent), 3/4 of 2/3 = 1/2 of the original remains. So 1/2 of the total = ₹900 → total = ₹1,800.
50. **B) 15** — Let the ages be 2*x* and 3*x*. After 5 years: (2*x*+5)/(3*x*+5) = 3/4 → 4(2*x*+5) = 3(3*x*+5) → 8*x*+20 = 9*x*+15 → *x* = 5. The elder's present age = 3×5 = 15.
