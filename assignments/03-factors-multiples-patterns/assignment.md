# Week 3 — Factors, Multiples, HCF/LCM & Number Patterns

15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** Which of the following is a factor of 84?
A) 5  B) 7  C) 9  D) 11

**Q2.** Which of the following is a multiple of 13?
A) 39  B) 42  C) 45  D) 48

**Q3.** Find the HCF of 48 and 60.
A) 6  B) 8  C) 12  D) 24

**Q4.** Find the LCM of 12 and 18.
A) 24  B) 36  C) 48  D) 72

**Q5.** Which of these is a prime number?
A) 51  B) 57  C) 59  D) 63

**Q6.** How many factors does 36 have?
A) 7  B) 8  C) 9  D) 10

**Q7.** Find the smallest number that is a multiple of both 4 and 9.
A) 24  B) 36  C) 48  D) 18

**Q8.** Which number is NOT a multiple of 11?
A) 77  B) 99  C) 110  D) 115

**Q9.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 4, 9, 19, 39, ___
A) 69  B) 74  C) 79  D) 84

**Q10.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ___
A) 36  B) 40  C) 42  D) 44

**Q11.** Find the missing number in the pattern: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, ___
A) 42  B) 45  C) 49  D) 54

**Q12.** Find the HCF of 36, 54 and 90.
A) 6  B) 9  C) 18  D) 27

**Q13.** Ritu is packing 96 apples equally into baskets of 12. How many baskets does she need?
A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 9

**Q14.** Two bells ring every 18 and 24 minutes respectively. If they ring together at 9:00 a.m., when will they next ring together?
A) 10:00 a.m.  B) 10:12 a.m.  C) 10:18 a.m.  D) 10:24 a.m.

**Q15.** Find a number that is a factor of 60, a multiple of 5, and greater than 10.
A) 15  B) 8  C) 14  D) 25
