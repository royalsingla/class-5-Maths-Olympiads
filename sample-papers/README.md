# Sample Papers

Full-length papers built to the **exact SOF IMO Level‑1 pattern**
(see `../docs/02-exam-pattern.md`):

- 50 questions, 60 marks, 60 minutes
- Section 1: Logical Reasoning — 15 Q × 1 mark
- Section 2: Mathematical Reasoning — 20 Q × 1 mark
- Section 3: Everyday Mathematics — 10 Q × 1 mark
- Section 4: Achievers (HOTS) — 5 Q × 3 marks
- No negative marking, 4-option MCQ throughout

This is a bigger paper than Class 4's (35 Q / 40 marks) — the 50/60 shape
is what Class 5 and above use.

Each paper ships as two files: `sample-paper-NN.md` (questions only) and
`sample-paper-NN-answer-key.md` (answers + one-line explanations).

## How to run one

1. Set a timer for **60 minutes**. No calculator, no notes.
2. Attempt all 50 questions. Skip and circle anything you're unsure of
   rather than stalling — come back if time remains.
3. Mark against the answer key. Score out of 60.
4. For every wrong answer, log the topic and the actual mistake (silly
   slip vs. concept gap) — that's what improves the score on the next
   paper, not just repetition.
