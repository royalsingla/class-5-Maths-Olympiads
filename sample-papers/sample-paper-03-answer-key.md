# Sample Paper 3 — Answer Key

Total: 60 marks (45 × 1 mark + 5 × 3 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **D) Shirt** — Hammer, Saw, and Nail are tools; a Shirt is not.
2. **A) Ship** — A pilot commands an airplane; a captain commands a ship.
3. **A) VSWI** — The rule is "+4 letters": R→V, O→S, S→W, E→I.
4. **C) 112** — Each term doubles: 7, 14, 28, 56, 112.
5. **B) 19th** — Position from the left = 32 − 14 + 1 = 19.
6. **C) West** — Two 90° anticlockwise turns from East = 180° turn = West.
7. **A) 4, 2, 3, 1** — Natural education order: Kindergarten → Primary School → High School → University.
8. **C) 23** — 12, 18, 30 are multiples of 6; 23 is not.
9. **B) Monday** — 31 days = 4 full weeks (28 days, same day: Friday) + 3 more days → Sat, Sun, Mon.
10. **B) Cousin** — Sonia's father's brother is her paternal uncle; his son is her cousin.
11. **C) 159** — Each term is "double and add 1": 4×2+1=9, 9×2+1=19, 19×2+1=39, 39×2+1=79, 79×2+1=159.
12. **A) OQPGA** — The rule is "+2 letters" (Y wraps to A): M→O, O→Q, N→P, E→G, Y→A.
13. **C) Square** — Cube, Cuboid, and Pyramid are 3-D solids; a Square is a flat (2-D) shape.
14. **D) Arjun** — Putting it together: Neha > Kabir > Meera > Arjun, so Arjun is lowest.
15. **C) 125** — These are cube numbers: 1³, 2³, 3³, 4³, 5³ = 1, 8, 27, 64, 125.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

25. **C) 117** — 65% of 180 = 0.65 × 180 = 117.
26. **B) 45%** — 9/20 = 0.45 = 45%.
27. **C) 25** — 1 part = 40 ÷ 8 = 5; smaller = 5 parts = 25.
28. **B) 10%** — Loss = 540−486 = ₹54; loss % = 54/540 × 100 = 10%.
29. **C) ₹1,080** — SI = (6000×9×2)÷100 = ₹1,080.
30. **A) 3,120** — 3 L = 3,000 mL; + 120 mL = 3,120 mL.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

36. **B) 33,300** — 1,850 × 18 = 33,300.
37. **A) ₹4,150** — 9500 − 3200 − 2150 = 4,150.
38. **C) 180 L** — 3/4 of 720 = 540 L used; 720 − 540 = 180 L left.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (3 marks each)

46. **C) 300** — If the original number is *x*: 1.3*x* = 390 → *x* = 300.
47. **B) 33** — Three consecutive odd numbers *n*−2, *n*, *n*+2 sum to 3*n* = 99 → *n* = 33 (the middle number).
48. **C) ₹8,000** — SI = (P×6×5)÷100 = 0.3×P = 2,400 → P = 2,400÷0.3 = ₹8,000.
49. **C) ₹3,000** — After rent, 3/5 of the money remains. After groceries (1/3 of that spent), 2/3 of 3/5 = 2/5 of the original remains. So 2/5 of the total = ₹1,200 → total = ₹3,000.
50. **C) 24** — Let the ages be 4*x* and 7*x*. After 6 years: (4*x*+6)/(7*x*+6) = 5/8 → 8(4*x*+6) = 5(7*x*+6) → 32*x*+48 = 35*x*+30 → *x* = 6. Younger's present age = 4×6 = 24.
