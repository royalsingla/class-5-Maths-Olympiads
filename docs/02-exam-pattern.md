# Exam Pattern — Class 5 Mathematics Olympiad

## 1. SOF IMO — Level 1 (the exam everyone takes)

| Section | # Questions | Marks each | Total marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Logical Reasoning | 15 | 1 | 15 |
| 2. Mathematical Reasoning | 20 | 1 | 20 |
| 3. Everyday Mathematics | 10 | 1 | 10 |
| 4. Achievers Section (HOTS) | 5 | 3 | 15 |
| **Total** | **50** | — | **60** |

- **Duration:** 60 minutes.
- **Question type:** Objective, 4-option MCQ.
- **Negative marking:** None.
- **Difficulty shape:** Same idea as Class 4 — Sections 1–3 are mostly
  single-step; Achievers is 2–3 step HOTS and worth **3 marks each** (vs.
  2 marks at Class 4) — even more leveraged for a good score and for
  tie-breaks.
- This is bigger than Class 4's paper (35 Q / 40 marks) — Class 4 and
  below use the 35/40 shape; Class 5 and above (through at least Class 10)
  use this 50/60 shape.

## 2. SOF IMO — Level 2 (for qualifiers only)

- Same 4-section structure and 60-minute duration, materially harder —
  more multi-step reasoning throughout, including in Sections 2 and 3.
- **Who qualifies for Level 2** (any one of these, class-wise/zone-wise):
  - Top 5% of students in that class, zone-wise, in Level 1.
  - Top 25 rank holders, zone-wise and class-wise.
  - The class topper, if ≥10 students from that class appeared and the
    topper scored ≥50%.
- **2025–26 Level‑2 cutoff for Class 5:** 42/60 total, with section
  minimums around Achievers (M4) 12, Mathematical Reasoning (M2) 13,
  Everyday Mathematics (M3) 9, Logical Reasoning (M1) 8 — treat as a
  target, not a guarantee, since cutoffs are revised each year.
- **Tie-break order** when totals are equal: Achievers → Mathematical
  Reasoning → Everyday Mathematics → Logical Reasoning (same priority
  order as Class 4) — Achievers is the highest-leverage section twice
  over: triple marks, and first tie-breaker.

## 3. Silverzone iOM — Class 5

30 questions, 40 minutes, 100 marks total. Sections: Mathematics (units
below), Reasoning and Aptitude, and Scholar's Zone (HOTS).

| Unit | # Questions | Marks each | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numbers and Their Operations | 5 | 3 | 15 |
| Parts and Whole (fractions/decimals) | 3 | 3 | 9 |
| Percentage, Ratio and Proportion | 4 | 3 | 12 |
| Geometry and Measurement | 6 | 3 | 18 |
| Graphical Representation of Data | 2 | 3 | 6 |
| Reasoning and Aptitude | 5 | 3.5 | 17.5 |
| Scholar's Zone (HOTS) | 5 | 4.5 | 22.5 |

No negative marking. English only.

## 4. Unified Council UIMO — Class 5

| Section | Marks |
|---|---|
| Mathematics | 35 |
| Logical Reasoning | 10 |
| Critical Thinking (HOTS) | 5 |
| **Total (50 questions)** | **50** |

Objective MCQs, no negative marking. Maths syllabus explicitly includes
ratios, speed/distance/time, simple interest, and profit/loss — all new
compared to the Class 4 UIMO paper.

## 5. What this means for practice

Same shape as Class 4's three exams, just scaled up: strictly timed,
all-MCQ, zero-negative-marking, with the same four ingredients (arithmetic
→ applied word problems → reasoning puzzles → multi-step HOTS) in
different proportions, plus two genuinely new topic areas
(percentage/ratio/proportion and profit-loss/simple-interest). The
practice plan and sample paper here are built to the **SOF IMO shape**
(50 Q / 60 marks / 60 min, 4 sections) since it's the strictest superset.
