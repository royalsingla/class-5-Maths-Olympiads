# CBSE Class 12 Physics — Important Derivations

A chapter-wise checklist of the derivations most likely to appear in the
CBSE Class 12 Physics board exam — the ones that repeat, year after year,
as 2-mark, 3-mark, or 5-mark questions. This is a **list to work through**,
not the derivations themselves — the idea is to use it as a revision
tracker: can you reproduce each one on paper, from the starting principle,
without looking it up?

> **Note on currency:** CBSE revises (rationalises) the Physics syllabus
> most years, and has dropped several derivations in recent cycles —
> Cyclotron, Velocity Selector, the Toroid, the magnetic dipole moment of
> a revolving electron, Earth's Magnetism, Eddy Currents, and Brewster's
> Law are **not** in this list because they were removed from the syllabus
> in past rationalisations. Cross-check against the current year's
> official CBSE curriculum (cbseacademic.nic.in) before finalising a
> revision plan, since the exact deleted/restored list can shift year to
> year.

**Frequency key:** ★★★ = asked almost every year, in some form · ★★ =
asked frequently · ★ = asked occasionally, still worth knowing.

## How to use this list

1. Go unit by unit. For each derivation, close the book and try to produce
   it from the starting principle (Coulomb's law, Gauss's law,
   Biot–Savart law, Snell's law, Bohr's postulates, etc.) through to the
   final result, with the diagram.
2. Mark anything you couldn't do cleanly — those go on a short list for
   a second pass closer to the exam.
3. The ★★★ items are worth over-preparing: they show up almost every
   year, sometimes as the derivation itself, sometimes as a numerical
   built on top of it.
4. Board papers often ask you to **derive, then apply** (e.g. derive the
   lens maker's formula, then use it in a numerical) — so pair each
   derivation with a couple of numericals on the same formula while
   revising.

Sources consulted for frequency/scope: recent-year CBSE sample papers and
previous-year question papers, and the current rationalised CBSE Class 12
Physics syllabus (cbseacademic.nic.in).
