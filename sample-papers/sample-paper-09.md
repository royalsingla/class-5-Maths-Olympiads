# Sample Paper 9 — Class 5 Mathematics Olympiad (Level‑2 Difficulty Step-Up)

**Pattern:** Same 50 questions / 60 marks / 60 minutes / no negative
marking as Papers 1–8, but noticeably harder throughout — closer to SOF
IMO **Level 2**.

---

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning (Q1–Q15, 1 mark each)

**Q1.** Find the odd one out:
A) Hockey  B) Football  C) Cricket  D) Chess

**Q2.** Doctor is to Patient as Teacher is to ___.
A) Student  B) School  C) Lesson  D) Book

**Q3.** If each letter of a word is moved 1 step forward in the alphabet, CAT becomes DBU. Using the same rule, what does HORSE become?
A) IPSTF  B) IPSTE  C) IQSTF  D) JPSTF

**Q4.** This series follows two interleaved patterns — the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th terms, and the 2nd, 4th, 6th, 8th terms. Find the missing (8th) term: 5, 100, 10, 93, 15, 86, 20, ___
A) 77  B) 78  C) 79  D) 80

**Q5.** Five friends — Rhea, Sameer, Tanvi, Umesh, Vikas — sit in a row. Tanvi sits exactly in the middle. Vikas sits at the left end. Umesh sits immediately to the right of Tanvi. Sameer sits between Vikas and Tanvi. Find the seating order, left to right.
A) Vikas, Sameer, Tanvi, Umesh, Rhea
B) Vikas, Tanvi, Sameer, Umesh, Rhea
C) Sameer, Vikas, Tanvi, Umesh, Rhea
D) Vikas, Sameer, Umesh, Tanvi, Rhea

**Q6.** Anita walks 16 m North, then 9 m further North, then 7 m South. How far is she from her starting point, and in which direction?
A) 16 m North  B) 18 m North  C) 20 m North  D) 25 m North

**Q7.** If the 11th day of a month is a Thursday, what day of the week is the 30th of the same month?
A) Monday  B) Tuesday  C) Wednesday  D) Sunday

**Q8.** Find the odd one out: A) 21  B) 28  C) 42  D) 45
(Hint: three of these are multiples of the same number.)

**Q9.** Find the next number: 5, 10, 40, 200, ___
A) 1,000  B) 1,100  C) 1,200  D) 1,300

**Q10.** In a queue, Farah is 12th from the front and 18th from the back. If 4 people leave from the back end, what will be her new position from the back?
A) 12th  B) 13th  C) 14th  D) 15th

**Q11.** Find the odd one out:
A) 13  B) 29  C) 39  D) 43

**Q12.** In a certain code, BOOK is written as ERRN (each letter moved 3 steps forward). How is PENCIL written in the same code?
A) SHQFLO  B) SHQFLP  C) SHRFLO  D) THQFLO

**Q13.** Find the odd one out:
A) Pentagon  B) Hexagon  C) Octagon  D) Cylinder

**Q14.** Four friends ran a race. Priya finished before Rina but after Sana. Tina finished after Rina. Who finished last?
A) Priya  B) Rina  C) Sana  D) Tina

**Q15.** Study the pattern: 3, 4, 6, 9, 13, ___. Find the next number.
A) 17  B) 18  C) 19  D) 20

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning (Q16–Q35, 1 mark each)

**Q16.** 4,89,675 + 3,78,456 = ?
A) 8,68,131  B) 8,67,131  C) 8,68,031  D) 8,69,131

**Q17.** 7,60,000 − 4,89,675 = ?
A) 2,70,325  B) 2,69,325  C) 2,71,325  D) 2,70,225

**Q18.** 6,543 × 14 = ?
A) 91,602  B) 91,502  C) 91,702  D) 92,602

**Q19.** 97,776 ÷ 18 = ?
A) 5,432  B) 5,422  C) 5,442  D) 5,332

**Q20.** Find the HCF of 108 and 144.
A) 18  B) 24  C) 36  D) 72

**Q21.** Find the LCM of 16 and 24.
A) 24  B) 36  C) 48  D) 96

**Q22.** Which of these fractions is the greatest: 8/9, 5/6, 13/15, 10/11?
A) 8/9  B) 5/6  C) 13/15  D) 10/11

**Q23.** 6 km 250 m + 4 km 900 m = ?
A) 10 km 150 m  B) 11 km 50 m  C) 11 km 150 m  D) 10 km 950 m

**Q24.** A rectangular field is 50 m long and 34 m wide. Find its area.
A) 1,680 sq m  B) 1,690 sq m  C) 1,700 sq m  D) 1,710 sq m

**Q25.** A regular nonagon (9 sides) has a perimeter of 108 cm. Find the length of each side.
A) 10 cm  B) 11 cm  C) 12 cm  D) 13 cm

**Q26.** The cost price of an item is ₹1,800 and the selling price is ₹2,250. Find the profit percentage.
A) 20%  B) 22.5%  C) 25%  D) 27.5%

**Q27.** A sum of money doubles itself in 10 years at simple interest. Find the rate of interest per annum.
A) 8%  B) 10%  C) 12%  D) 15%

**Q28.** ₹600 is divided among three people in the ratio 3:4:5. Find the largest share.
A) ₹225  B) ₹240  C) ₹250  D) ₹260

**Q29.** Convert 0.45 to a fraction in simplest form.
A) 9/20  B) 4/9  C) 9/25  D) 5/11

**Q30.** 7 kg 300 g + 5 kg 650 g + 4 kg 550 g = ?
A) 16 kg 500 g  B) 17 kg  C) 17 kg 500 g  D) 18 kg

**Q31.** How many seconds are there in 5.5 hours?
A) 18,900  B) 19,400  C) 19,800  D) 20,200

**Q32.** Find the perimeter of an equilateral triangle with side 38 cm.
A) 76 cm  B) 104 cm  C) 114 cm  D) 38 cm

**Q33.** Find the area of a rectangle with length 55 cm and breadth 36 cm.
A) 1,960 sq cm  B) 1,970 sq cm  C) 1,980 sq cm  D) 1,990 sq cm

**Q34.** How many faces does a triangular pyramid (tetrahedron) have?
A) 3  B) 4  C) 5  D) 6

**Q35.** Find the number of diagonals in a nonagon (9 sides).
A) 24  B) 25  C) 26  D) 27

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics (Q36–Q45, 1 mark each)

**Q36.** A school collected ₹3,15,000 from 7 classes equally. How much did each class contribute?
A) ₹44,000  B) ₹44,500  C) ₹45,000  D) ₹45,500

**Q37.** A factory makes 3,600 bottles in 10 hours, at a steady rate. How many bottles does it make in 7 hours?
A) 2,420  B) 2,470  C) 2,520  D) 2,570

**Q38.** A tank holds 6,000 L and is filled by a pipe at 200 L per minute. How long will it take to fill the tank completely?
A) 28 min  B) 29 min  C) 30 min  D) 32 min

**Q39.** A bus leaves city A at 6:45 a.m., stops for 25 minutes on the way, and reaches city B at 12:00 noon. How much time did the bus spend actually travelling (excluding the stop)?
A) 4 h 30 min  B) 4 h 40 min  C) 4 h 50 min  D) 5 h

**Q40.** A chemist mixes 6 L 500 mL of one liquid with 3 L 500 mL of another, then pours the mixture equally into 5 bottles. How much does each bottle get?
A) 1 L 800 mL  B) 1 L 900 mL  C) 2 L  D) 2 L 100 mL

**Q41.** Rohit buys 6 notebooks at ₹42 each and gets a discount of ₹22 on the total bill. How much does he pay?
A) ₹220  B) ₹225  C) ₹230  D) ₹235

**Q42.** A cistern with 2,100 L of water is used for irrigation at the rate of 1/6 of its original capacity every day. How much water remains after 3 days?
A) 1,000 L  B) 1,050 L  C) 1,100 L  D) 1,150 L

**Q43.** A delivery van carries 4 boxes weighing 23 kg 400 g, 19 kg 600 g, 16 kg 250 g, and 12 kg 750 g. Find the total weight carried.
A) 71 kg  B) 71 kg 500 g  C) 72 kg  D) 72 kg 500 g

**Q44.** 2,352 chairs are arranged equally into rows of 42 chairs each. How many rows will there be?
A) 52  B) 54  C) 56  D) 58

**Q45.** A trader bought 85 kg of wheat for ₹3,400 and sold all of it at ₹46 per kg. Find his total profit.
A) ₹470  B) ₹490  C) ₹510  D) ₹530

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (Q46–Q50, 3 marks each)

**Q46.** A number is first multiplied by 8, then 32 is subtracted from the result, giving 144. Find the original number.
A) 20  B) 21  C) 22  D) 23

**Q47.** The sum of three numbers is 225. The second number is twice the first, and the third is 25 more than the second. Find the first number.
A) 36  B) 38  C) 40  D) 42

**Q48.** Priyanka had a certain amount of money. She spent 1/3 of it on books, then 1/5 of what remained on stationery, and was left with ₹1,200. How much money did she have at first?
A) ₹2,100  B) ₹2,175  C) ₹2,250  D) ₹2,325

**Q49.** In a school, the number of girls is 9 more than twice the number of boys. There are 354 students in total, and 115 of them are boys. Find the number of girls.
A) 233  B) 236  C) 239  D) 242

**Q50.** A sequence starts with 4 and 7, and each later term is the sum of the two terms before it: 4, 7, 11, 18, 29, ___. Find the next term.
A) 41  B) 44  C) 47  D) 50
