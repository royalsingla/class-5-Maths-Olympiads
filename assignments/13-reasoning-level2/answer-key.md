# Logical Reasoning — Level 2 — Answer Key

1. **A) Jaya, Ishaan, Kabir, Lata, Mohan, Nisha** — Jaya is at the left end, position 1. Kabir is third from the left, position 3. Lata is immediately right of Kabir, position 4. Nisha is at the right end, position 6. Mohan sits between Lata (4) and Nisha (6), so Mohan is position 5. The only person left, Ishaan, takes position 2.
2. **A) SPDO** — Shift each letter 3 steps forward: L→O, A→D, M→P, P→S, giving "ODPS"; then reverse it: "SPDO".
3. **A) Father** — Vikram is Anita's husband, and Anita is Rohit's mother, so Vikram is Rohit's father.
4. **A) Karan** — The order from lowest to highest is Nitin, Leena, Karan, Mira, so Karan scored second-highest.
5. **D) LINEN** — LISTEN, SILENT, and ENLIST are all made of the same six letters (L, I, S, T, E, N) rearranged; LINEN uses different letters, so it isn't an anagram of the other three.
6. **A) 23-25-18** — S is the 19th letter (19+4=23), U is the 21st letter (21+4=25), N is the 14th letter (14+4=18).
7. **B) Tuesday** — From 20th June to 15th July is (30−20) + 15 = 25 days later. 25 = 3 weeks + 4 days, so the day of the week moves forward 4 days from Friday: Tuesday.
8. **C) 159** — Each term is double the previous term plus 1: 39×2+1 = 79, 79×2+1 = 159.
9. **A) Chess, Pune** — Isha plays Badminton. Since Dev doesn't play Chess, Dev must play Tennis, leaving Chess for Farah. The Tennis player lives in Nashik, so Dev lives in Nashik. Farah doesn't live in Nagpur, so (with Nashik taken) Farah must live in Pune, leaving Nagpur for Isha.
10. **B) Grandfather** — Arjun is Meera's father, and Deepak is Arjun's father, so Deepak is Meera's grandfather.
11. **A) 6 m East** — Starting facing North: turning right means now facing East (6 m East); turning right again means now facing South (10 m South). The two North/South legs cancel (10 m each way), leaving a net 6 m East of the starting point.
12. **C) Both I and II follow** — "All squares are rectangles" and "All rectangles are quadrilaterals" chain together to give "All squares are quadrilaterals" (Conclusion I). Since squares are a real category here, it also follows that at least some quadrilaterals are squares (Conclusion II).
13. **A) 1, 3, 5, 2, 4** — The natural life cycle is: Seed → Plant → Tree → Flower → Fruit.
14. **C) 14** — Replace each symbol with its meaning: 20 **÷** 4 **×** 3 **−** 2 **+** 1. By order of operations: 20÷4 = 5, 5×3 = 15, then 15−2+1 = 14.
15. **C) 64** — The rule is "square the number": 6² = 36, so 8² = 64.

---

**How to use this:** 10+/15 is strong for a Level-2-focused set. The
seating puzzle (Q1), the logic grid (Q9), and the syllogism (Q12) are the
three hardest — if any of those tripped you up, re-read the clues one at a
time and write down what each one rules out before moving to the next,
rather than trying to hold the whole puzzle in your head.
