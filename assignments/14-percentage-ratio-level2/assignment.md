# Percentage, Ratio & Profit/Loss — Level 2 (Multi-Step)

A dedicated harder follow-on to Week 5, for students who've qualified
Level 1. This whole topic is new versus Class 4, and Level 2 leans hard on
it — chained percentage changes, combined ratios, and profit/loss problems
with an extra step. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** A number is first increased by 25%, then the result is decreased by 20%. If the final value is 600, find the original number.
A) 550  B) 575  C) 600  D) 625

**Q2.** Three numbers are in the ratio 3:4:7. The difference between the largest and smallest numbers is 48. Find the sum of the three numbers.
A) 154  B) 161  C) 168  D) 175

**Q3.** If A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5, find A:C.
A) 2:5  B) 8:15  C) 6:10  D) 4:5

**Q4.** A shopkeeper marks an item 40% above its cost price of ₹500, then sells it after a discount of 15% on the marked price. Find his profit percentage.
A) 15%  B) 17%  C) 19%  D) 21%

**Q5.** A sum amounts to ₹5,200 in 3 years and to ₹6,000 in 5 years at simple interest. Find the rate of interest per annum.
A) 8%  B) 9%  C) 10%  D) 12%

**Q6.** Find 20% of 30% of 5,000.
A) 200  B) 250  C) 300  D) 350

**Q7.** Two numbers are in the ratio 3:5. If 10 is added to each number, the new ratio becomes 2:3. Find the two original numbers.
A) 24, 40  B) 27, 45  C) 30, 50  D) 33, 55

**Q8.** A man sells an article for ₹800, gaining an amount that is 15% of the selling price. Find the cost price of the article.
A) ₹660  B) ₹670  C) ₹680  D) ₹690

**Q9.** In how many years will a sum of money become 4 times itself at 15% per annum simple interest?
A) 15 years  B) 18 years  C) 20 years  D) 24 years

**Q10.** 40% of a number is 25% more than 96. Find the number.
A) 280  B) 290  C) 300  D) 310

**Q11.** An article is sold for ₹456 at a loss of 5%. Find its cost price.
A) ₹470  B) ₹475  C) ₹480  D) ₹485

**Q12.** The ages of two brothers are in the ratio 4:5. After 6 years, the ratio of their ages will be 5:6. Find their present ages.
A) 20, 25  B) 22, 27  C) 24, 30  D) 28, 35

**Q13.** Find the simple interest on ₹8,000 at 9% per annum for 2 years 6 months.
A) ₹1,700  B) ₹1,750  C) ₹1,800  D) ₹1,850

**Q14.** A number is increased by 30%, and then the result is increased by a further 20%. Find the overall percentage increase.
A) 50%  B) 52%  C) 54%  D) 56%

**Q15.** A man buys a bicycle for ₹2,400 and spends ₹300 on repairs. He then sells it at a profit of 20% on his total cost. Find the selling price.
A) ₹3,120  B) ₹3,180  C) ₹3,240  D) ₹3,300
