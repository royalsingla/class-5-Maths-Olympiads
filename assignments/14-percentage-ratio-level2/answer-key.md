# Percentage, Ratio & Profit/Loss — Level 2 — Answer Key

1. **C) 600** — A 25% increase multiplies by 1.25; a 20% decrease then multiplies by 0.80. Combined factor: 1.25 × 0.80 = 1.00 exactly — so the final value always equals the original. Original = 600.
2. **C) 168** — The largest and smallest parts differ by 7−3 = 4 parts, equal to 48, so 1 part = 12. Sum of all parts = (3+4+7) × 12 = 14 × 12 = 168.
3. **B) 8:15** — Scale A:B = 2:3 to 8:12 (×4) and B:C = 4:5 to 12:15 (×3), so both share B = 12. Then A:B:C = 8:12:15, giving A:C = 8:15.
4. **C) 19%** — Marked price = 500 × 1.40 = ₹700. Selling price after 15% discount = 700 × 0.85 = ₹595. Profit = 595−500 = ₹95. Profit % = 95/500 × 100 = 19%.
5. **C) 10%** — The interest earned between year 3 and year 5 is ₹6,000−₹5,200 = ₹800 over 2 years, so ₹400 per year. Principal = ₹5,200 − (3×₹400) = ₹4,000. Rate = (400×100)/4,000 = 10%.
6. **C) 300** — 20% of 30% of 5,000 = 0.20 × 0.30 × 5,000 = 300.
7. **C) 30, 50** — Let the numbers be 3*x* and 5*x*. (3*x*+10)/(5*x*+10) = 2/3 → 3(3*x*+10) = 2(5*x*+10) → 9*x*+30 = 10*x*+20 → *x* = 10. Numbers = 30 and 50.
8. **C) ₹680** — Gain = 15% of the selling price = 15% of ₹800 = ₹120. Cost price = selling price − gain = ₹800 − ₹120 = ₹680.
9. **C) 20 years** — Becoming 4 times itself means the interest earned equals 3 times the principal. Using SI = (P×R×T)÷100 with interest = 3P: 3P = (P×15×T)÷100 → T = 300÷15 = 20 years.
10. **C) 300** — 25% more than 96 = 96 × 1.25 = 120. If 40% of the number is 120, the number = 120 ÷ 0.40 = 300.
11. **C) ₹480** — If ₹456 represents a 5% loss, it is 95% of the cost price. Cost price = 456 ÷ 0.95 = ₹480.
12. **C) 24, 30** — Let the ages be 4*x* and 5*x*. (4*x*+6)/(5*x*+6) = 5/6 → 6(4*x*+6) = 5(5*x*+6) → 24*x*+36 = 25*x*+30 → *x* = 6. Ages = 24 and 30.
13. **C) ₹1,800** — Time = 2 years 6 months = 2.5 years. SI = (8,000 × 9 × 2.5) ÷ 100 = ₹1,800.
14. **D) 56%** — Combined factor = 1.30 × 1.20 = 1.56, i.e. a 56% overall increase.
15. **C) ₹3,240** — Total cost = ₹2,400 + ₹300 = ₹2,700. Profit = 20% of ₹2,700 = ₹540. Selling price = 2,700+540 = ₹3,240.

---

**How to use this:** 10+/15 is strong for a Level-2-focused set. Q1 and
Q14 are worth re-reading carefully even if you got them right — spotting
that "+25% then −20%" cancels out exactly, while "+30% then +20%" does
*not* simply add to 50%, is exactly the kind of percentage-chain trap
Level 2 papers use.
