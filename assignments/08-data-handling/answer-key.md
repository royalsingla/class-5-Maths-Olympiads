# Week 8 — Data Handling — Answer Key

**Q1. D) 13** — 2 groups × 5 = 10, plus 3 extra strokes = 13.

**Q2. C) Wednesday** — Wednesday shows 4 symbols × 20 = 80 books, the fewest.

**Q3. C) 700** — Monday (100) + Tuesday (140) + Wednesday (80) + Thursday (160) + Friday (120) = 700.

**Q4. A) 80** — Thursday = 8 symbols × 20 = 160 books; Wednesday = 4 symbols × 20 = 80 books; difference = 160 − 80 = 80.

**Q5. C) 140** — Total (700) ÷ 5 days = 140.

**Q6. C) Tina** — Tina scored 96, the highest in the graph.

**Q7. C) 30** — Highest (96) − lowest (66) = 30.

**Q8. B) 3** — Aman (84), Rohan (90), and Tina (96) all scored above 80.

**Q9. B) 486** — 84+72+90+66+96+78 = 486.

**Q10. C) 81** — 486 ÷ 6 = 81.

**Q11. B) 200** — 8 × 25 = 200.

**Q12. C) 40** — Tea = 35% × 200 = 70; coffee = 45% × 200 = 90; juice = 200 − 70 − 90 = 40.

**Q13. D) April** — April had 77 mm, the highest.

**Q14. B) 220 mm** — 45 + 60 + 38 + 77 = 220.

**Q15. C) 22** — Average = 220 ÷ 4 = 55; difference from highest (77) = 77 − 55 = 22.
