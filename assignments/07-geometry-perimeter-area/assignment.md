# Week 7 — Geometry, Symmetry, Perimeter & Area

15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** How many sides does an octagon have?
A) 6  B) 7  C) 8  D) 9

**Q2.** Which of these shapes has no straight sides?
A) Pentagon  B) Circle  C) Hexagon  D) Rectangle

**Q3.** How many lines of symmetry does an equilateral triangle have?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 4

**Q4.** Which type of angle measures exactly 90°?
A) Acute  B) Right  C) Obtuse  D) Straight

**Q5.** Which type of angle is greater than 90° but less than 180°?
A) Acute  B) Right  C) Obtuse  D) Reflex

**Q6.** Find the perimeter of a square with side 15 cm.
A) 45 cm  B) 60 cm  C) 75 cm  D) 225 cm

**Q7.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle with length 22 cm and breadth 14 cm.
A) 36 cm  B) 72 cm  C) 144 cm  D) 308 cm

**Q8.** A square has a perimeter of 48 cm. Find the length of each side.
A) 10 cm  B) 12 cm  C) 14 cm  D) 16 cm

**Q9.** Find the area of a rectangle with length 18 cm and breadth 11 cm.
A) 188 sq cm  B) 198 sq cm  C) 208 sq cm  D) 58 sq cm

**Q10.** Find the area of a square with side 16 cm.
A) 246 sq cm  B) 256 sq cm  C) 64 sq cm  D) 266 sq cm

**Q11.** A rectangular garden has an area of 168 sq m. If its length is 14 m, find its breadth.
A) 10 m  B) 12 m  C) 14 m  D) 16 m

**Q12.** How many right angles make a straight angle (a straight line)?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 4

**Q13.** Which of these solids has exactly 1 curved surface and 1 flat circular face?
A) Sphere  B) Cone  C) Cylinder  D) Cube

**Q14.** How many edges does a cuboid (rectangular box) have?
A) 6  B) 8  C) 10  D) 12

**Q15.** A square garden of side 20 m has a path 2 m wide running all around it on the outside. Find the perimeter of the outer boundary of the path.
A) 80 m  B) 88 m  C) 96 m  D) 104 m
