# Achievers — Level 2 (Multi-Step HOTS)

A dedicated harder follow-on to Week 11, for students who've qualified
Level 1 and are prepping for Level 2. Every question needs two or more
genuine reasoning steps — working backwards, combining two facts, chaining
a percentage/ratio/SI calculation, or solving for an unknown before using
it. 15 questions, 4-option MCQ, untimed.

---

**Q1.** A number is increased by 20% to become 360. This new number is then decreased by 25%. Find the final value.
A) 215  B) 220  C) 225  D) 230

**Q2.** Three numbers are in the ratio 2:3:5. Their sum is 250. Find the largest number.
A) 100  B) 110  C) 120  D) 125

**Q3.** Priya had some money. She spent 2/7 of it on rent, then 15% of what remained on groceries, and was left with ₹2,550. How much money did she have at first?
A) ₹3,900  B) ₹4,050  C) ₹4,200  D) ₹4,350

**Q4.** A mother's age is 3 times her daughter's age. After 10 years, the mother will be twice the daughter's age. Find the daughter's current age.
A) 8  B) 9  C) 10  D) 12

**Q5.** Pipe A can fill a tank in 10 hours. Pipe B can fill the same tank in 15 hours. If both pipes are opened together, how long will they take to fill the tank?
A) 5 hours  B) 6 hours  C) 7 hours  D) 8 hours

**Q6.** The sum of four consecutive odd numbers is 120. Find the largest of the four numbers.
A) 29  B) 31  C) 33  D) 35

**Q7.** The sum of two numbers is 134, and their difference is 26. Find the larger number.
A) 76  B) 78  C) 80  D) 82

**Q8.** Meena has only ₹10 and ₹5 coins. She has 22 coins in total, worth ₹170 altogether. How many ₹10 coins does she have?
A) 10  B) 11  C) 12  D) 13

**Q9.** A train covers 300 km in 5 hours at a steady speed. At the same speed, how long will it take to cover 450 km?
A) 6 h 30 min  B) 7 hours  C) 7 h 30 min  D) 8 hours

**Q10.** A two-digit number is 4 times the sum of its digits. The ones digit is 2 more than the tens digit. Find the number.
A) 15  B) 24  C) 36  D) 42

**Q11.** The length of a rectangle is 8 cm more than its breadth. If the perimeter is 84 cm, find the area of the rectangle.
A) 405 sq cm  B) 416 sq cm  C) 425 sq cm  D) 432 sq cm

**Q12.** Find the next number: 3, 8, 18, 38, 78, ___
A) 148  B) 152  C) 155  D) 158

**Q13.** A shopkeeper marks the price of an item at ₹2,000, then gives two successive discounts of 10% and 5%. Find the final selling price.
A) ₹1,690  B) ₹1,700  C) ₹1,710  D) ₹1,720

**Q14.** A sum of money amounts to ₹7,000 in 4 years and to ₹8,000 in 6 years at simple interest. Find the principal.
A) ₹4,800  B) ₹4,900  C) ₹5,000  D) ₹5,100

**Q15.** A number leaves a remainder of 4 when divided by 7, and a remainder of 3 when divided by 5. The number is between 100 and 150. Find the number.
A) 109  B) 116  C) 123  D) 131
