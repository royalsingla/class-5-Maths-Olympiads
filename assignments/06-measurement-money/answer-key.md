# Week 6 — Measurement & Money — Answer Key

**Q1. B) 7,500 m** — 1 km = 1000 m, so 7.5 km = 7,500 m.

**Q2. A) 3 kg 450 g** — 3450 g = 3000 g + 450 g = 3 kg 450 g.

**Q3. B) 2,500 mL** — 1 L = 1000 mL, so 2.5 L = 2,500 mL.

**Q4. B) 9 km 350 m** — 600 m + 750 m = 1350 m = 1 km 350 m; 5 km + 3 km + 1 km 350 m = 9 km 350 m.

**Q5. A) 7 kg 350 g** — 12 kg = 11 kg 1000 g; 11 kg 1000 g − 4 kg 650 g = 7 kg 350 g.

**Q6. B) 2 h 30 min** — 150 ÷ 60 = 2 remainder 30, so 2 hours 30 minutes.

**Q7. C) 5°C** — The smallest value is the coldest: 5°C.

**Q8. B) ₹244** — (3×₹45) + (2×₹12) + ₹85 = ₹135 + ₹24 + ₹85 = ₹244.

**Q9. B) 6** — 1800 mL ÷ 300 mL = 6 glasses.

**Q10. B) 395 L** — 850 − 275 − 180 = 395 L.

**Q11. C) 6 h 30 min** — 8:20 a.m. to 2:20 p.m. is 6 hours; plus 30 more minutes to 2:50 p.m.

**Q12. B) 2.5 kg** — 12.5 ÷ 5 = 2.5 kg.

**Q13. B) 5 kg 850 g** — 15,250 g − 9,400 g = 5,850 g = 5 kg 850 g.

**Q14. B) 9:20 p.m.** — 6:45 p.m. + 2 h = 8:45 p.m.; + 35 more minutes = 9:20 p.m.

**Q15. B) ₹2,000** — 40 L = 40,000 mL; 40,000 ÷ 500 = 80 packets; 80 × ₹25 = ₹2,000.
