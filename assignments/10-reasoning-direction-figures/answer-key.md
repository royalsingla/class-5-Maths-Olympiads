# Week 10 — Answer Key

## Part A — Ranking, Direction Sense, Clock & Calendar

**Q1. B) 20th** — Position from the right = total − position from left + 1 = 30 − 11 + 1 = 20.

**Q2. B) 40** — Total = 15 + 26 − 1 = 40.

**Q3. B) Green** — Order from top to bottom: Yellow, Red, Green, Blue, Black — Green is the middle (3rd of 5).

**Q4. A) East** — South (180°) rotated 90° anticlockwise (subtract 90°) = 90° = East.

**Q5. A) 9 km East** — Net East distance = 8 + 6 − 5 = 9 km East.

**Q6. A) East** — The rule rotates each direction 90° clockwise (East→South, South→West); applying the same rule, North→East.

**Q7. B) Tuesday** — 23 days = 3 full weeks (21 days, same day: Thursday) + 2 more days back → Wednesday, Tuesday.

**Q8. B) Friday** — 25 − 1 = 24 days later; 24 ÷ 7 leaves remainder 3, so count 3 days forward from Tuesday: Wednesday, Thursday, Friday.

**Q9. C) 8:35** — 7:45 + 50 minutes = 8:35.

**Q10. C) Team D** — The chain is A > B > C > D, and E is highest of all — that doesn't change who's lowest. D is the lowest.

## Part B — Mirror Images, Water Images & Embedded Figures

**Q11. C** — Option C correctly mirrors P left-right: the bowl of the P flips to the other side of the vertical stroke.

**Q12. A** — Option A correctly mirrors R left-right.

**Q13. D** — Option D correctly mirrors the digit 2 left-right.

> For every mirror question: check each wrong option against what it
> actually is — the figure unchanged, flipped top-to-bottom instead of
> left-right, or spun 180°. Only a true left-right flip is a mirror image.

**Q14. B** — "b" flipped upside-down (its true water image) looks like "p". (Option A is unchanged; C is "b" mirrored left-right, which looks like "d"; D is "b" rotated 180°, which looks like "q". The four letters b/d/p/q are exactly the four ways of flipping one shape — a good way to remember the difference between a mirror image and a water image.)

**Q15. D** — Option D correctly flips G upside-down (its true water image).

**Q16. C) Pentagon** — The composite figure is a square with a pentagon overlapping its top — the pentagon's outline is clearly traceable within it.
