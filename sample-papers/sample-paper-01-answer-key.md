# Sample Paper 1 — Answer Key

Total: 60 marks (45 × 1 mark + 5 × 3 marks), 60 minutes.

## Section 1 — Logical Reasoning

1. **C) Circle** — Square, Rectangle, and Triangle are polygons (straight sides); a Circle is not.
2. **B) Animal** — Rose belongs to the group "Flower"; Lion belongs to the group "Animal".
3. **A) DBU** — The rule is "+1 letter": C→D, A→B, T→U.
4. **C) 34** — The gaps increase by 1 each time (+4, +5, +6, +7, +8): 4, 8, 13, 19, 26, 34.
5. **B) 13th** — Position from the right = total − position from left + 1 = 20 − 8 + 1 = 13.
6. **B) South** — Two 90° clockwise turns from North = 180° turn = South.
7. **A) 1, 5, 2, 3, 4** — Natural growth order: Seed → Sapling → Tree → Flower → Fruit.
8. **C) 21** — 17, 19, 23 are prime numbers; 21 = 3 × 7 is not.
9. **B) Saturday** — 17 days = 2 full weeks (14 days, same day: Wednesday) + 3 more days → Thursday, Friday, Saturday.
10. **B) Cousin** — Sita's mother's only brother is Sita's maternal uncle; his son is Sita's cousin.
11. **C) 47** — The pattern is "double and add 1": 2×2+1=5, 5×2+1=11, 11×2+1=23, 23×2+1=47.
12. **A) VCDNG** — The rule is "+2 letters": T→V, A→C, B→D, L→N, E→G.
13. **D) Square** — Cube, Sphere, and Cone are 3-D solids; a Square is a flat (2-D) shape.
14. **D) D** — Putting it together: D > A > B > C, so D is the tallest.
15. **C) 36** — These are square numbers (1², 2², 3², 4², 5², 6²): 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36.

## Section 2 — Mathematical Reasoning

16. **A) 69,137** — 45,678 + 23,459 = 69,137.
17. **A) 45,438** — 80,005 − 34,567 = 45,438.
18. **A) 27,648** — 3,456 × 8 = 27,648.
19. **A) 1,368** — 9,576 ÷ 7 = 1,368 exactly.
20. **C) 12** — Factors of 24: 1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24. Factors of 36: 1,2,3,4,6,9,12,18,36. HCF = 12.
21. **A) 24** — Multiples of 6: 6,12,18,24... Multiples of 8: 8,16,24... First common one = 24.
22. **A) 6/8** — 6/8 simplifies to 3/4.
23. **A) 4/5** — Same denominator: add the numerators: 2+1+1=4, so 4/5.
24. **A) 3/4** — 0.75 = 75/100, which simplifies to 3/4.
25. **C) 30** — 15% of 200 = 0.15 × 200 = 30.
26. **C) 75%** — 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.
27. **C) 18** — 2 parts = 12 pens, so 1 part = 6; pencils = 3 parts = 18.
28. **B) 25%** — Profit = ₹100 − ₹80 = ₹20; profit % = 20/80 × 100 = 25%.
29. **B) ₹100** — Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100 = (1000 × 5 × 2) ÷ 100 = ₹100.
30. **A) 4250 g** — 4 kg = 4000 g; 4000 + 250 = 4250 g.
31. **C) 210 min** — 3.5 × 60 = 210 minutes.
32. **B) 46 cm** — Perimeter = 2 × (14 + 9) = 46 cm.
33. **C) 144 sq cm** — Area = side × side = 12 × 12 = 144 sq cm.
34. **B) 2** — A (non-square) rectangle has 2 lines of symmetry.
35. **C) 4** — A full turn is 360°; 360 ÷ 90 = 4 right angles.

## Section 3 — Everyday Mathematics

36. **A) 70,500** — 2,350 × 30 = 70,500.
37. **A) ₹2,875** — 5000 − 1250 − 875 = 2875.
38. **B) 300 L** — 1/3 of 450 = 150 L used; 450 − 150 = 300 L left.
39. **C) ₹50** — 1250 ÷ 25 = 50.
40. **B) 336** — 8 × 42 = 336.
41. **C) ₹360** — SI = (2000 × 6 × 3) ÷ 100 = ₹360.
42. **C) 20%** — Profit = 2880 − 2400 = ₹480; profit % = 480/2400 × 100 = 20%.
43. **C) 150 km** — Speed = 60 km/h; time = 2.5 h; distance = 60 × 2.5 = 150 km.
44. **B) 3 cups** — 3/4 × 4 = 3 cups.
45. **C) 90** — 60% of 150 = 0.6 × 150 = 90.

## Section 4 — Achievers Section (3 marks each)

46. **C) 200** — If the original number is *x*: 1.2*x* = 240 → *x* = 200.
47. **C) 26** — Three consecutive even numbers summing to 72: let them be *n*−2, *n*, *n*+2; their sum is 3*n* = 72, so *n* = 24, and the largest is 26.
48. **B) ₹6,000** — SI = (P × R × T) ÷ 100 → 1200 = (P × 5 × 4) ÷ 100 = P × 0.2 → P = 1200 ÷ 0.2 = ₹6,000.
49. **B) ₹1,200** — After the book, 3/4 of the money remains. After the pen (1/3 of that remainder spent), 2/3 of 3/4 = 1/2 of the original remains. So 1/2 of the total = ₹600 → total = ₹1,200.
50. **B) 12** — Let A = 3*x*, B = 5*x*. After 4 years: (3*x*+4)/(5*x*+4) = 4/6. Cross-multiplying: 3(3*x*+4) = 2(5*x*+4) → 9*x*+12 = 10*x*+8 → *x* = 4. So A = 3×4 = 12. Check: A=12→16, B=20→24 after 4 years; 16:24 = 2:3 = 4:6. ✓
